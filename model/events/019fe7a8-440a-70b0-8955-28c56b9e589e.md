## Notes

RECOVERED BY THE RE-SWEEP — the sweeps named it in DataCommandRejectedOutOfSequence's notes as a folded sibling and never made it an event. It is the second of the RFC's 'only two points' at which DATA can fail, and it is the same condition as the first (no usable envelope) discovered at the OTHER end of the content transfer, after the server has already read a whole message it will discard. That difference is the whole reason to keep both. ⚠️ CITATION ERROR IN THE INHERITED MATERIAL, corrected here: this passage is §3.3, at lines 1137-1142 of the file, NOT §4.1.1.4 — the section attribution above reads 4.1.1.4 only because the harvest carried it that way; the heading at line 1015 is §3.3 and §3.4 begins at 1162, so lines 1137-1142 sit inside §3.3. Strength: "should fail only if" — an odd construction that constrains WHEN failure is legitimate rather than permitting failure, and not a 2119 SHOULD in the usual sense. The third cause the same sentence names, resources unavailable, is the sweeps' ResourcesBecameUnavailable and belongs to Server Configuration; the second, policy, is the next event.
