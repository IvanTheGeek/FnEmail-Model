# `LayeringSemanticsPreservedAcrossBoundary`

A gateway carried the sending environment's envelope-and-content layering through to the receiving environment's own layering, rather than flattening one into the other.

## Data

**No data.** The method sanctions this: *"If an event has no meaningful payload beyond its identity (e.g., a simple state transition), it is fine to have no fields or just the identity key."* It also warns, of a different case, that *"An event without fields is an opaque label"* — both are recorded here rather than reconciled, because reconciling them is a later step's work, not Brain Storming's.

## Where RFC 5321 says so

| Section | Lines | Text |
|:--|:--|:--|
| §E | 5130-5132 | *"In general, gateways between the Internet and other mail systems SHOULD attempt to preserve any layering semantics across the boundaries between the two mail systems involved."* |
| §E | 5132-5136 | *"Gateway-translation approaches that attempt to take shortcuts by mapping (such as mapping envelope information from one system to the message header section or body of another) have generally proven to be inadequate in important ways."* |
