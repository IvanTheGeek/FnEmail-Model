## Notes

RECOVERED BY THE RE-SWEEP — the violated arm of the queueing MUST, which no inherited candidate carried. It is the one violated arm in this chapter whose consequence is message loss rather than delay, which is why it earns a slot rather than a line in a sibling's notes. Never stated in the RFC; inferred strictly as the negation of the MUST at 3840-3842, hence likely rather than certain. Opposite event: MessageQueuedAfterTemporaryDnsError. Ambiguity: because the RFC never says how long 'later' extends, an implementation that queues for sixty seconds and then fails is hard to distinguish from one that never queued — 🟥 on the same boundary that QueueLifetimeExpired sits on in another chapter.
