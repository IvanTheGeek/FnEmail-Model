## Notes

CITATION CORRECTED during the merge: the inherited candidate cited §3.9 lines 1692-1693 for "the address of a person or other entity who administers the list"; line 1692 ends at "MUST be changed to be" and the quoted phrase sits entirely on line 1693. Narrowed to 1693. Included because it is the value ReturnPathRewrittenToListAdministrator writes into every envelope: change it and every message already in flight keeps the old value while new ones get the new one, which is a real consistency question, not a hypothetical. The RFC is careful to say "a person or other entity", so this need not be a human and often is a role address, which is why the owner- prefix example is used. Rejected alternative names: ListOwnerChanged, AdministratorMailboxReassigned, BounceAddressForListChanged. This is the candidate the condemned status/phase register would catch by shape alone — an X-Changed event with a before and an after — and the register is explicitly not in force during Step 1; both authors' worked material contains AddressChanged and PriceChanged of exactly this shape.
