## Notes

Harvested from #287 into this chapter because 1524 explicitly routes the reader to 6.4 - the two sections state the same prohibition from different ends, and the repair refusal is the 'content left untouched' rule at its sharpest. Strength: MUST NOT. The sharpest role distinction in the spec: the identical edit is permitted to an ORIGINATING server and forbidden to a RELAY, which makes the actor part of the event's identity rather than metadata on it. Whether a refusal is an event or merely the absence of one is a genuine Step 1 question; kept because the role determination that produces it is a real fork. Siblings from the reject-versus-repair debate at 4078-4081 - MalformedMessageRejected, MalformedMessagePassedOnUnchanged - carry no normative strength at all; the RFC says the debate 'shows no signs of abating'. Rejected names: MalformedMessagePassedOnUnchanged, RelayDeclinedToRepair. --- FIELD HYGIENE: struck queue_id (Message-rooted; a minted value with no bearing on a repair refusal) and relay_host. refused_repairs survives with a genuine source: 6.4 lines 4131-4135 name the three changes. Stated precisely, since the audit hunted message_id fields: the RFC's message-id here is a header field an ORIGINATING server may add (4127-4131) and a relay MUST NOT (4140-4141) - the subject of a prohibition, not an identity key on an event.

FIELD HYGIENE PASS (chapter 14): message_id struck, for a reason specific to this event rather than a generic one. RFC 5321 supplies no message identifier a receiver can rely on: section 4.4 line 3336's ID clause is optional, sits inside a trace header, and admits an Atom — typically the receiving system's own queue id — as readily as an RFC 5322 msg-id, so it is not a key on the message. Worse, in this event the message is by hypothesis malformed and the missing message-id may be exactly what the relay is refusing to supply (line 4131), so keying the event on it assumes the very thing that is absent. Identity here is the message on this hop, and section 4.4's By-domain clause at line 3319 is where the RFC records which hop — which is why relay_host is KEPT, with its value corrected from mx.bar.com, absent from the RFC, to bar.com, which Appendix D names as a host at 4881 and its trace line uses at 5064. refused_repair KEPT and sourced to the three bulleted repairs at 4131, 4133 and 4135.
