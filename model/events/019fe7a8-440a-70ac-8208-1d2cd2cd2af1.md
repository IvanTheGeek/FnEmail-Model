## Notes

RECOVERED BY THE RE-SWEEP, and explicitly requested by the sweep that found TerminatingLineEndingAdded: "the other, rejecting the message as invalid, is a sibling candidate MessageRejectedByOriginator that a later step should recover". Named MessageRejectedAsInvalidByOriginator here rather than MessageRejectedByOriginator so that the reason travels with the name; the sweep's form is recorded as the rejected alternative, along with SubmissionRejectedAsInvalid. Strength: MUST, second arm — see TerminatingLineEndingAdded for the shape. What makes it worth its own sticky rather than a flag on the other arm: the outcomes have nothing in common. One produces a message on the wire with an extra octet pair; the other produces no SMTP session at all and a report back to a human user, on a channel RFC 5321 never describes. Screens: inbound-only scope and the responsibility-boundary cutoff, same as its sibling. Opposite event: TerminatingLineEndingAdded.
