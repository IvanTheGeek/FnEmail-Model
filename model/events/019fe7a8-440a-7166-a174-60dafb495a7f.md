## Notes

RECOVERED BY THE RE-SWEEP. §7.3 names two non-conformant ways to fake VRFY support — the false 250 at 4247-4248 and the blanket 550 at 4249-4251 — and the inherited set carried neither. Strength: no 2119 keyword in this sentence; 'is equally not in conformance' is a flat statement of fact, and the MUST NOT it elaborates is at 4241. Anyone tiering this on modal strength must read the case, not hunt for a keyword. Rejected alternative names: BlanketVerificationDenial, AlwaysNegativeVrfyAnswered, VerificationFakedByRefusal. Sibling and opposite: VerificationFalselyClaimed is the same dishonesty in the positive direction; VerificationDeclinedAsDisabled is the CONFORMANT way to decline, and the contrast between them — 252 conformant, blanket 550 not — is the cleanest statement in the chapter of why the code chosen carries meaning independent of the answer. Do not merge with ExpnAccessDenied (a real access decision about a real list) or CrossTypeVerificationRefused (a real finding about the entry's kind): those 550s are earned, this one is not. Which screen would have killed it: the consumer test, the HELO-only charter, and the 'only facts' rule via the same objection raised on VerificationFalselyClaimed. FIELD HYGIENE PASS: struck reply_code (550). Wire rendering — and here doubly so, because the whole point of the event is that the 550 was returned with no checking behind it, so the code encodes nothing whatever about the address. The 252-conformant/blanket-550-not contrast the note above draws lives in the meaning and the §7.3 references, not in a field. server_host repeats the actor and is flagged for a later step; it is kept because it is sourceable and not derived, and striking it would be a modeling judgment rather than hygiene. No invented identity key was present; identity is the query and the channel.
