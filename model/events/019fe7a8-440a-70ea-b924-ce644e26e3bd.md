## Notes

Configurability itself is a MUST (3713-3714) — one of the strongest statements in the span, and it only makes sense if a configuring event exists. Three different strengths sit on the three values: SHOULD on the interval, no keyword at all on the give-up time, MAY on the shorter notification budget, and the separate notification_max_retries field is what makes the MAY visible in the payload.
