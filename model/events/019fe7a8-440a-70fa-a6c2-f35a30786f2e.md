## Notes

Recovered by the re-sweep: the inherited MessageRequeuedForExtensionSupport named this sibling in its notes as the other arm of the and/or and never gave it a slot. MAY strength. It overlaps the address-resolution chapter's multihomed and MX-ordering material (§5.1 3874-3876, 3893-3896) but is not the same fact — there the client moves on because an address failed, here because a reachable server lacks a capability, and the second reason is invisible to any model built only from connection outcomes. Cross-referenced to that chapter rather than merged. Screens: inbound-only scope, HELO-only charter. ⚠️ FIELD HYGIENE PASS: NOTHING STRUCK.
