## Notes

RECOVERED BY THE RE-SWEEP, and the chapter charter names it in as many words: "list promoted to full MUA when it modifies". The §3.9 sweep set it aside explicitly — "Deciding whether a list "needs to be viewed as" a full MUA (§3.9.2 lines 1745-1746) — a judgment about behavior, derivable from whether MessageModifiedByList occurred, so a fold over events rather than an event" — and that reasoning is recorded here rather than overturned: it is the strongest argument against this candidate and it may well win at a later step. Kept for Step 1 on two grounds. First, the classification has consequences the fold does not carry: once a list is a full MUA, DeliveryAcceptedByList and MessagePostedByList exist as separate messages with separate identities, and where the classification lands decides the event count for every later message. Second, in practice a list is configured to modify before any message arrives, which makes this an administrative fact with a time and an actor rather than a derived one. Marked doubtful to keep the tension visible. Rejected alternative names: ListPromotedToFullMua, ListBecameMailUserAgent, ListClassifiedAsEndpoint. No RFC 2119 keyword: "need to be viewed as" (1745).
