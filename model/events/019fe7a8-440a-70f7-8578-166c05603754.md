## Notes

Recovered by the re-sweep — named in an inherited span's cut pile and never modeled. MAY on supporting concurrency at all, and only "may be appropriate" on the limit, so this is the weakest normative ground in the chapter: two permissions stacked. The stated purpose is host self-protection, not politeness to the receiver, which distinguishes it from the receiving-side connection limit at §4.5.4.2 3778-3781 (the session-lifecycle chapter's material, not claimed here). Sibling not given a slot: ConcurrentOutgoingTransactionStarted, the positive arm, which is arguably just a session start. Screens: inbound-only scope, altitude tiers. ⚠️ FIELD HYGIENE PASS: STRUCK client_host — RFC 5321 gives a host "one or more areas for queuing messages in transit" (rfc5321.txt:3673-3675), so the host does not identify a queue, and the spec supplies no queue identifier; the same strike was made on QueueRunStarted. What carries identity here is the deferred queue entry, on §4.4's ID-clause ground (rfc5321.txt:3336), and f2C8D14 is this project's minted value rather than the RFC's. concurrency_limit stays because the ceiling is the whole content of the event, but its value is labeled illustrative: the RFC declines to give a number, saying only that some limit "may be appropriate". No count of transactions in flight is carried — that is a fold over the open transactions and belongs in a read model.
