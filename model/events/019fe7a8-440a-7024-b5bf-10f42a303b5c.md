## Notes

RECOVERED BY THE RE-SWEEP — named as an uncarried sibling of MailExchangerRecordPublished in the inherited notes. The precondition for TargetNameResolvedToCanonicalName, and the constraint at 716-718 is what makes the whole chase legitimate: a CNAME is permitted only where its target 'can be resolved, in turn, to MX or address RRs'. The SAME act published at the wrong place — a CNAME at an MX target rather than at a queried name — is the defect at 3859-3862, which is MailExchangerTargetFoundUnresolvable; one publication event, two opposite verdicts decided purely by where the record sits, which is worth carrying.
