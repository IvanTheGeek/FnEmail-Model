## Notes

Recovered by the re-sweep from §4.5.4's opening paragraph, which no inherited candidate touched — every sweep jumped straight to §4.5.4.1. MUST strength, and note the scope: "all activities", "per-command basis", so the timer is per command inside a queued delivery attempt and not one timer over the attempt. The violated arm — a queue activity that ran with no timeout at all — is real and is folded here rather than given its own slot; a facilitator may want it split. Related but distinct: the §4.5.3.2 client timeout family, which is the same discipline stated for a live session rather than for a queuing strategy, and which belongs to the timeouts chapter. Screens: inbound-only scope, altitude tiers. ⚠️ FIELD HYGIENE PASS: STRUCK destination_host — it restates the queue entry's destination and adds nothing the identity key does not already reach; the MUST at 3681-3682 is scoped per command, not per destination. command_verb is therefore the one fact that makes this event meaningful, and RCPT is the RFC's own token from the minimum-implementation list at rfc5321.txt:3387. queue_entry_id kept on §4.4's ID-clause ground (rfc5321.txt:3336); f2C8D14 is this project's minted value, not the RFC's.
