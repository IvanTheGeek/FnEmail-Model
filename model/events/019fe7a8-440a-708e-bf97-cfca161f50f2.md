## Notes

Held out of CommandTimeoutExpired deliberately, because the consequence is categorically different and the RFC says so at length: the server is typically mid-delivery when the final period lands, so a spurious timeout here produces duplicate delivery rather than a lost attempt. This is the responsibility-transfer boundary seen from the client's side of the ambiguity — the one moment in SMTP where the two ends can legitimately disagree about whether a message exists. Ten minutes is the longest timeout in §4.5.3.2 and this is exactly why. The spec points onward to §6.1 for the fuller discussion. Screens: FnEmail's responsibility-boundary cutoff fails hardest here, since the whole point is that the boundary is momentarily indeterminate; the inbound-only scope kills it as a client event.
