## Notes

FOUR candidates merged into one state change: ClientIdentityAsserted (§2 sweep), ClientIdentityDeclared (§3.2 sweep), ClientIdentityDeclaredWithoutExtensions (§3.2 sweep, HELO arm) and ClientIdentityAnnounced (§4.1.1.1 sweep). Rejected alternative names carried in full: ClientIdentityAsserted, ClientIdentityAnnounced, ClientIdentityDeclaredWithoutExtensions, ClientIdentified, ExtensionCapabilityDeclared, HelloIssued. The HELO arm was folded rather than kept separate because the state change is identical — the server now holds a claimed name; what differs is greeting_verb, and its consequences are carried by SessionInitialStateConfirmed and ServiceExtensionsAdvertised. A facilitator may legitimately split on greeting_verb: EHLO carries a second fact HELO does not — that the client can process service extensions (§3.2 lines 989-992) — and the one-event-per-command design target is what forced the fold. The RFC says 'identifies itself', never 'proves': bar.com at 203.0.113.20 may be a lie the protocol cannot catch here. greeting_verb is load-bearing — the ABNF admits an address literal in EHLO (line 1829) but NOT in HELO (line 1831), so a nameless HELO client has no conforming argument at all. Strengths: MUST for a client issuing one before a transaction; MUST for servers supporting HELO (lines 1818-1819); SHOULD for starting with EHLO (lines 1812-1813). Screens: the HELO-only charter kills the EHLO arm and hides that ABNF asymmetry. FIELD HYGIENE PASS: struck 2 fields. session_id struck — no such key in RFC 5321; identity on this event is the claim itself plus its position in the session, and the claimed name is exactly what the server holds afterwards. client_address struck — the address belongs to the channel and was recorded at TransmissionChannelOpened; pairing it with the claim is the business of ClientNameVerificationRecorded, which §4.1.4 lines 2481-2485 makes a separate, optional act. Striking it sharpens the note above: the declaration is unverified by construction, and nothing in this event contradicts the claim.
