## Notes

MAY strength, and the RFC introduces it by conceding that its own requirement is over-broad ('Despite the apparent scope of this requirement', 1039). The inherited candidate carried an acceptability_deferred Boolean; DROPPED here as padding — the deferral is the event's identity, and the kit warns against padding events to reach a count. Screens that would have killed it, all named and none applied: the consumer test G1 (nothing drawn consumes it), the capture/decision two-kinds lens (no decision is taken at this step, which is the entire point), and 'one event per command' (two different 250-producing events on one MAIL). Its consequence is now a separate event rather than a lost note — see ReversePathProblemReportedAfterForwardPathsExamined.

FIELD HYGIENE PASS: struck one field, reply_code (250), as the wire rendering of a decision. The same reasoning that already removed acceptability_deferred as padding removes this: the deferral is the event's identity, and the 250 is what makes this event and ReversePathAccepted indistinguishable on the wire, so it was the least informative fact available. §3.3 1042-1045 states the code and is cited above. No identity key was present; identity is the reverse-path plus position in the session — this is the MAIL of the open transaction.
