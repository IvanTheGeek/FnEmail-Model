## Notes

Distinct from ServiceShutdownDetected — there the server decides, here something external decides for it — and the distinction has teeth because the obligations differ, not merely the narrative. The word 'attempt' is doing real work: the RFC concedes the notice may not get out, and that failure arm surfaces on the client as ConnectionLostWithoutQuit rather than as ShutdownNoticeReceived, so the two client-side outcomes of one server-side event are unlike each other. 'before exiting' implies a process-exit event too; it was considered and dropped as pure infrastructure with nothing but a timestamp, but it is what bounds how long the notice has to complete. Rejected alternative names: ServerProcessTerminated, ServiceStoppedExternally. Screens: the altitude tiers and the inbound-only scope.
