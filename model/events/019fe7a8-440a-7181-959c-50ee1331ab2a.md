## Notes

The permission at 3407 is a lowercase 'may', not the RFC 2119 MAY — verified against the line, and it matters, because a lowercase may is weaker and this one overrides a genuine MUST from 3394-3396. The narrow-tailoring constraint immediately after IS an uppercase SHOULD. Reporting either as 'MAY' would lose the asymmetry. blocked_source instantiated with 203.0.113.20 per rule 8 makes the narrow-tailoring SHOULD checkable: blocking one source is tailored, blocking all Postmaster traffic at foo.com is not — the placeholder version of this field cannot be judged against the SHOULD at all. Precondition with no SMTP command behind it: DenialOfServiceAttackDetected / SecurityBreachDetected, an operator or monitoring-system event. Screens: the condemned status/phase register, the consumer test, and the inbound-only scope's silence on operator action. FIELD HYGIENE: nothing struck. reason is not padding and not a constant — 3406-3407 names two grounds, a denial of service attack 'or other breach of security', and it is the justification that licenses overriding a MUST, so it is one of the one-or-two facts that make the event meaningful. Its value is verbatim from line 3406. No invented identity key was present; identity is the domain plus the blocked source, and RFC 5321 supplies no session or connection identifier to strike.
