## Notes

RECOVERED BY THE RE-SWEEP; no inherited candidate carried it. Deliberately distinct from the inbound concurrency ceiling on SmtpListenerStarted (§4.5.4.2 3778-3779): outbound transactions and inbound TCP connections are different resources with opposite failure modes — too few outbound and the queue drains slowly, too few inbound and legitimate senders are turned away. Both are MAY, and neither is named as an event by the RFC. 'some limit may be appropriate' at 3770-3771 is lowercase and hedged twice; there is no number anywhere, so 25 is illustrative and marked as such rather than presented as a spec value. FIELD HYGIENE: nothing struck; both fields are essential and the sweep had already labeled the 25 as illustrative in prose — that label is now carried on the field itself, where the next reader will see it. Opposite arm the RFC also offers: no limit at all, which is what 'MAY support multiple concurrent' permits. No session, transaction or connection key was present; identity is the client host named by its domain, and note that the transactions this ceiling counts are precisely the objects RFC 5321 gives no identifier to — the count exists, the handles do not.
