## Notes

RECOVERED BY THE RE-SWEEP — three separate sentences across §4.2, §4.2.1, §4.3.1 and §4.3.2 all prohibit or discourage the same act and no inherited candidate carried it. It is the violated arm of ReplyTextCustomized: the RFC's bargain is that a receiver MAY change the text freely and MUST NOT change what a code means, and this event is the bargain broken. Strengths, deliberately varied in the source: lowercase "should not" at 2730 (not an RFC 2119 keyword), SHOULD at 3021-3022, SHOULD at 3051-3054 pointing to RFC 3463 enhanced status codes as the sanctioned alternative. Note the legitimate arm, which is not this event: new codes MAY be added by a Standards-Track document (2620-2623), so the vocabulary is extensible through a process, just not by an operator — a facilitator may want ReplyCodeVocabularyExtended as a separate Extension Registry event, and it is named here rather than given a row. Rejected alternative names: UndocumentedReplyCodeUsed, ReplyCodeMeaningAltered. Screens: the four altitude tiers (Infrastructure) and G1.

FIELD HYGIENE PASS (chapter 14): nothing struck — this event carried no session key and no f2C8D14. invented_code is the event's own content rather than a rendering: lines 2730-2732 make the act of inventing a code the fault, so the code is the datum. server_host is sourced to Appendix D. situation stays illustrative and that is expected — the RFC is describing behavior it discourages and gives no example of the occasion for it.
