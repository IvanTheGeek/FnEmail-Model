## Notes

RECOVERED BY THE RE-SWEEP. The violated arm of the MUST at 3206-3207; no inherited candidate carried it. It is not a bookkeeping violation: §6.1 3973-3975 names the Return-Path line as one of the two places a later notifier looks for the address to bounce to, so an omission here disarms the failure-reporting half of the obligation accepted at ResponsibilityForMessageAccepted. That is the whole chapter's spine broken at one point, which is why the arm deserves a row rather than a footnote. Confidence 'likely' because the RFC states the rule and never states the violation as an occurrence. Sibling in the opposite direction: MessageDeliveredWithAmbiguousReturnPath, below — too few and too many are different failures with different consequences, so they are kept apart. ⚠️ FIELD HYGIENE PASS 2026-08-09 — one field struck. message_id: RFC 5321 supplies no message identifier — §4.4's ID clause (line 3336) is optional and belongs to the receiver's own Received: stamp, msg-id is RFC 5322's — and f2C8D14 is this project's minted queue_id value. The absence bites hard on this event in particular: a delivered message with no Return-Path line and no identifier has nothing in it that points back at the transaction that carried it, which is exactly the damage §6.1 3973-3975 describes. Identity is the delivered message's recipient and the host that delivered it.
