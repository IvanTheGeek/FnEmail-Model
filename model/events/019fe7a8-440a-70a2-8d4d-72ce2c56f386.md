# `EightBitOctetsNormalized`

## Where RFC 5321 says so

| Section | Lines | Text |
|:--|:--|:--|
| §4.5.2 | 3444-3448 | *"If the transmission channel provides an 8-bit byte (octet) data stream, the 7-bit ASCII codes are transmitted, right justified, in the octets, with the high-order bits cleared to zero. | §4.5.2 | 3441 | *"The mail data may contain any of the 128 ASCII characters."* |
