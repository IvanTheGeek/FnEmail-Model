## Notes

ONE EVENT PER RECIPIENT. The RFC says the step 'can be repeated any number of times' (1055) and that each instance names exactly one recipient (1061), which is the whole basis for this chapter's 'judged one at a time' framing. stream_root is Recipient rather than Transaction because the judgment arms below all attach to the individual forward-path, not to the transaction. Note the hedge at 1060, 'normally a mailbox and domain' — the RFC hedges because a forward-path 'can contain more than just a mailbox' (1076), which is the source-route case. NOTIFY=SUCCESS is a real RFC 3461 DSN parameter, legitimate only if the server offered DSN in its EHLO response — see the held-over UnnegotiatedParameterTransmitted for the MUST NOT that governs it. Rejected alternative names: ForwardPathDeclared, RecipientOffered, RcptIssued.

FIELD HYGIENE PASS: struck one field, declared_at. RFC 5321 places a date-time in exactly one construct, the Received stamp at §4.4 3311-3313, which belongs to the trace chapter; RCPT carries no time, so occurrence time here is ambient metadata rather than payload, and the example was not even the RFC's own ('Thu, 21 May 1998 05:33:29 -0700' at 5014). forward_path survives and is the identity of this event and of the Recipient stream it roots — the RFC gives no recipient identifier either, and the forward-path is what distinguishes one RCPT from the next. rcpt_parameters survives on the grammar at 1057, with its example now labeled plainly: NOTIFY is not in RFC 5321, a fact the note above already stated and the field's sourcing now carries.
