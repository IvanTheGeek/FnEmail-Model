## Notes

MAY strength, and doubly discretionary — "MAY choose, based on the specific extension and circumstances" (598-599). Note the and/or at 599: this and AlternateMailExchangerTried can both fire for one message, so they are not alternatives. Deliberately NOT merged with MessageRequeued: the trigger here is a capability mismatch discovered before any delivery failure, and it starts a second clock (the fallback timeout) that an ordinary requeue does not. Determining that the next hop lacks the extension is a lookup against the EHLO response and is in set_aside. Screens: inbound-only scope; the HELO-only charter is decisive, since a model that never issues EHLO can never discover the mismatch. ⚠️ FIELD HYGIENE PASS: STRUCK requeued_at as a bare occurrence stamp — the second clock the notes mention is the fallback timeout, whose elapsing is its own event (ExtensionFallbackTimeoutElapsed), so nothing here measures from this moment. required_extension and next_hop_host both come straight from the RFC's own sentence at 595-600, and 8BITMIME is the spec's own token at rfc5321.txt:922. queue_entry_id kept on §4.4's ID-clause ground (rfc5321.txt:3336); f2C8D14 is this project's minted value, not the RFC's.
