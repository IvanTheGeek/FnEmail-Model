## Notes

RECOVERED BY THE RE-SWEEP as a split from ExplicitSourceRouteGenerated, which had absorbed it. The inherited note itself said 'a future reader who wants both arms as separate events should split here' - the cap is why that was not done. Strength: MUST NOT, with NO escape hatch, against the SHOULD NOT plus escape hatch that governs generating a source route at all. Folding them hides the sharpest fact in 3.6.1. The sentence carries TWO forbidden acts joined by 'or', and the inherited set captured neither cleanly: generating an invalid route, and depending on serial resolution of names. The second is the practice at 1433-1436 seen from the client's side; a reader who wants it separate should split again and name it SerialNameResolutionRelied. Consequence: DeliveryFailedAfterSourceRouteStripped. Rejected names: InvalidRouteSubmitted, SerialNameResolutionRelied. --- FIELD HYGIENE: struck unresolvable_hop (a reading of the path). Genuine finding on the example: RFC 5321 forbids invalid source routes at 1437-1439 and never shows one, so no example can be cited. The value is built from the RFC's route form (1970) with a destination name of the kind described at 1433-1436, 'names that do not appear in the DNS as destination names', and is labeled illustrative. Citing the RFC's VALID route as an example of an invalid one would have been a fabricated citation.
