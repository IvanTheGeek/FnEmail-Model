## Notes

Merged from #78, #182 (4.1.1.3) and #271 (5.1); rejected names: MessageRelayedToNextHop, MessageForwardedToNextHop, RelayLegCompleted. Note the asymmetry 3.6.2 and 3.6.3 create: if this happens nothing further is required; if it cannot happen, 3.6.3's MUST fires and a notification is owed. RelayTaskAccepted therefore has exactly two terminal outcomes here. The forwarded reverse-path is UNCHANGED from the original (compare 4.1.1.3 lines 1943 and 1949, verified) - a relay does not become the sender. Do not merge with an originator's send: #271's three-way fork at 5.1 belongs to the routing chapter. Strength: declarative. Screens: inbound-only scope, responsibility-boundary cutoff. --- FIELD HYGIENE: struck relay_host and relayed_at. Three fields kept deliberately, not as padding: Appendix D.3 lines 5058-5060 put exactly this envelope on the onward wire, and the reverse-path stays because its being unchanged is the fact the event exists to carry - D.3 confirms it, the same \<JQP@bar.com> at 5008 on the inbound leg and 5058 on the onward one.
