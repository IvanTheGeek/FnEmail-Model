## Notes

Recovered by the re-sweep from a fold the inherited MessageAcceptedForProcessing (candidate 154) flagged but had no slot for. §3.3 states the 250-after-DATA TWICE, at 1105-1107 (after the text is 'successfully received and stored') and again at 1127 (after the server is told 'to now process the stored recipients and mail data'). Read strictly those are two different moments with only one 250 on the wire between them, and the split is the more faithful reading: storage of content is not acceptance of responsibility, and a server that stored the text and then failed policy processing has been in this state. Doubtful because nothing in the RFC turns on the distinction and no reply marks it — its only observable is the crash window between the two. Kept under the rule that Step 1 keeps what it is unsure of. Note the sentence at 1116-1127 crosses a page break in the source (footer at 1122, header at 1124), so the two fragments are quoted separately rather than joined.
