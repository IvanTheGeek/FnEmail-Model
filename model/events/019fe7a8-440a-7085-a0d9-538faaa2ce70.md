## Notes

Carried from the §3.8 sweep, split out of the fused SessionClosureRequested. RFC looseness worth preserving verbatim under rule 2: line 1643 says 'a positive reply code', line 1649 says 'a 221 reply', §4.1.1.10 line 2207 writes '221 OK', and the registered text at §4.2.2 line 2809 is a fourth form — four descriptions of one reply, and the general statement is weaker than the enumerated exception. The reply is load-bearing state and not a rendering: §3.8 licenses the close only 'After receiving a QUIT command and responding with a 221 reply', so its issuance is a precondition of the close rather than a display of it.
