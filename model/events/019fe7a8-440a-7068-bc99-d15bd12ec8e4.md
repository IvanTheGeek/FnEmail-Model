Inherited candidate 278, kept. MUST NOT — the refusal arm, and the cut that keeps bounce storms finite: because every notification is itself sent with a null reverse-path (3972-3973), a failure to deliver a notification hits this rule and stops. The two rules together are the whole loop-prevention mechanism of §6.1, and they only work as a pair. Cross-reference, do not merge: NotificationSuppressedToPreventLoop (inherited candidate 87, §3.6.3 line 1505-1506) is the relay-chapter twin — a different MUST NOT reached by a different route — and ErrorNotificationSuppressedToPreventLoop (candidate 350, §4.5.4) is a third.
