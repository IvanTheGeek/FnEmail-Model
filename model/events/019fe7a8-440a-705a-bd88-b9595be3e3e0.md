## Notes

Merged from four inherited candidates that named one state change from four sections: MessageDepositedInMessageStore (§2.3.3/§2.3.10), MessageInsertedIntoDestinationMailbox (§4.1.1.3), MessageDelivered (§4.2.5), and MessageDeliveredToMailbox. Rejected alternative names, all defensible: MessageInsertedIntoDestinationMailbox (the most RFC-literal, using the spec's own verb), MessageDelivered (the shortest, and the one a business reader would use), MessageDeliveredToMailbox, MailDropped. MessageDepositedInMessageStore wins because §2.3.11 828-831 makes 'mailbox' and 'depository' the same thing, so this name covers the §4.1.1.3 wording without losing the responsibility-transfer sense §2.3.3 gives it. The merge is justified because §2.3.11 identifies the mailbox with the store; a facilitator who thinks a host-convention insertion and a store deposit are different acts should split on host convention and cite the same lines. 'in accordance with its host mail conventions' is a deliberate handoff out of the protocol — the RFC declines to say what a mailbox is or how insertion works, and that is a 🟥 to mark rather than resolve. Note the RFC's own hedge at §4.2.5 2947, '(if the recipient mailbox exists)': acceptance obliges delivery, retry or notification, but never promises delivery. Sibling owned elsewhere: MessageDeliveredToTerminal (inherited candidate 319, Appendix F.6 lines 5231-5241, SEND/SOML/SAML), a genuinely different destination for delivery, left to whoever holds the obsolete-commands chapter. Screens: the responsibility-boundary cutoff, inbound-only scope, and the altitude tiers all stop short of this event; the actor 'message store' does not exist in the current model at all. ⚠️ FIELD HYGIENE PASS 2026-08-09 — two fields struck. message_id: RFC 5321 supplies no message identifier — §4.4's ID clause (line 3336) is optional and is the receiver's own trace clause, msg-id is RFC 5322's — and f2C8D14 is this project's minted queue_id value. Identity here is the destination mailbox, and §2.3.11 828-831 is unusually explicit that this is what identity means at this point: the address IS 'a location into which mail will be deposited'. deposited_at: struck as the event's own occurrence time.
