## Notes

Recovered by the re-sweep, and the clearest thing the cap stole: line 3709 states TWO terminal arms for the retry loop and every inherited candidate carried only the failure one (DeliveryGivenUp). A model with a give-up event and no success event has a queue that can only ever end badly. No normative keyword — it is the definition of when retries stop, not a requirement. Opposite event: DeliveryGivenUp. Rejected names: QueueEntryDischarged, MessageDequeued (the latter was listed in an inherited span's cut pile). Screens: inbound-only scope and the responsibility-boundary cutoff; the consumer test G1 would also press hard, since nothing in the current model reads the outcome of an outbound attempt. ⚠️ FIELD HYGIENE PASS: STRUCK transmitted_at — a bare occurrence stamp, and unlike attempted_at on DeliveryAttemptFailed no rule measures from it; 3709-3710 makes transmission the end of the clock rather than the start of one. destination_host is kept and is an observed fact here rather than a derived one: it is the host that actually accepted the message, not a lookup result. queue_entry_id kept on §4.4's ID-clause ground (rfc5321.txt:3336); f2C8D14 is this project's minted value, not the RFC's.
