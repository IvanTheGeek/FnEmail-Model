## Notes

A precondition nobody writes down. 'After detecting the need' makes the detection a prior, separate fact from the 421, and one detection governs every open session at once — which is why affected_sessions is genuinely a List and why this cannot live inside a per-session event. A named cause, not carried as its own event: resources becoming unavailable, which §3.3 mentions only in a parenthesis, 'including, of course, the server unexpectedly becoming unavailable'; the inherited candidate ResourcesBecameUnavailable covers it and belongs to the Transaction or Queue chapter, though it is the precondition behind both a 4yz on DATA and a connection dying mid-content. Kept apart from ServiceForciblyShutDown, not merged: the RFC keeps them in separate paragraphs at different strengths — self-detected shutdown gets an exception to a MUST NOT (permission), forcible shutdown gets a SHOULD (obligation). Screens: altitude tier G4 rules it Infrastructure (it exists because of how we run the software) and the inbound-only scope has no lane for it; both are wrong to apply in Step 1. That argument survives the strike but must now rest on the prose: one detection governs every open channel at once, and no identifier is needed to say so. server_domain survives as the identity of the service instance.
