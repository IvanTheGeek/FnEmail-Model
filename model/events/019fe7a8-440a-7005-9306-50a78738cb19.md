## Notes

Carried from the inherited candidate, unmerged. Strength differs by actor inside one sentence: SHOULD NOT for initial-submission servers, MUST NOT for relays, and RFC 4409 submission servers get 'somewhat more flexibility' — which is why inferring_role is an essential field and not padding; it is what decides whether the same state change is discouraged or forbidden. The honored arm (declining to infer) is a non-act with no event, so only the inference is recorded; the inherited sibling name FqdnInferenceRefusedByRelay is deliberately NOT carried for that reason. Rejected alternative names: PartialNameExpanded, LocalAliasResolvedToFqdn. The mechanism is declared out of scope at 3830-3831, so the payload is implementation-defined: 🟥. Which project screen would have killed it: the inbound-only scope and the HELO-only charter, since the actor is a submitting or relaying system, not the inbound receiver. ⚠️ FIELD HYGIENE PASS: nothing struck; no fabricated identity key was present. inferring_role is kept on the inherited argument — it changes the requirement's strength inside one sentence — but it does restate the `actor` slot at instance level, and that overlap is recorded here so a later step can decide whether the role belongs in the field list or only in the actor. inferred_fqdn takes the walk scene's bar.com; partial_name is the illustrative truncation of it, since RFC 5321 declares the inference mechanism out of scope at 3830-3831 and therefore supplies no example of one.
