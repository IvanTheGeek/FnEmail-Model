## Notes

RECOVERED BY THE RE-SWEEP — named only as a sibling in idx 194's notes, never given a row, and the §4.4 cap is why. It is the compliant, privacy-preserving path and the one §7.6 actually recommends ('with caution or not at all'), so a roster that carries only the narrowing arm makes the deprecated behavior look like the default. Strengths: the omission is permitted by grammar (Opt-info makes every clause optional, 3329-3330) and recommended by a *lowercase* should at §7.6 4327-4328 — not a 2119 SHOULD, and that distinction is exactly the kind rule 3 records being got wrong. The cost of omitting is that the trace no longer answers 'for whom', which is precisely what §4.4 lines 3184-3186 say trace exists to support. Opposites: TraceRecipientNarrowed, MultiplePathsStampedInForClause, BlindCopyRecipientDisclosedInTraceField. FnEmail screens: the consumer test / G1, and it is an absence rather than an act, which the condemned status/phase register would also have argued about. ⚠️ FIELD HYGIENE PASS: struck the fabricated `message_ref` identity key — RFC 5321 names no message identifier and f2C8D14 is not in the RFC; the forward-paths and the stamping host are what identify this hop's omission.
