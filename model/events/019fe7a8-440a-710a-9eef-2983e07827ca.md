## Notes

RECOVERED BY THE RE-SWEEP, from a clause the merged list cited only as background. Line 1183 states outright that the disclosed final address may be unreachable by the sender — so the 551 branch has a failure arm the section names but never handles, and it is one of §3.4's stated reasons for suppressing disclosure in the first place (a corrected address that leaks information AND does not work is pure cost). Marked DOUBTFUL: the RFC states the condition as a property, not as an occurrence, and gives it no reply code, no notification, and no remedy. It is offered rather than dropped under the kit's guidance to include when unsure and refine later, and because §3.4's whole argument leans on it.
