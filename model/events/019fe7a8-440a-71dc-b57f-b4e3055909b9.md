## Notes

MERGE of three inherited candidates naming one state change: AddressVerificationDisabled (7.3), VrfySupportDisabled and ExpnSupportDisabled (3.5.2). Names rejected: those three plus DirectoryCommandDisabled, VerificationServiceWithdrawn. The two-event split was argued on real grounds, preserved here: 1360-1361 says 'either or both', so they are independently switchable, and the consequences DIFFER — disabling EXPN also removes an EHLO extension line (MUST, 1364-1366) while VRFY MAY be listed but need not be (1366-1369). Three normative directions pull against each other: SHOULD support (1359), MAY provide a way to disable (1360), yet the 500/502 replies that disabling produces are called 'not in full compliance' at 3.5.3 lines 1378-1381, and 7.3 lines 4271-4273 add a standing SHOULD to keep EXPN. The spec sanctions a configuration it calls non-compliant: a genuine hotspot. Also the concrete case of 7.1's advocacy at 4207-4210. Screens that would have killed it: the condemned status register, the HELO-only charter, the altitude tiers. FIELD HYGIENE: struck reason (address harvesting by spammers). 3.5.2 line 1360 gives the motive as a category, 'For security reasons', and 7.3 discusses harvesting in prose; neither makes motive data the server holds, and free-text motive on a configuration act is commentary. command_disabled is genuinely two-valued per 'either or both'. Identity: RFC 5321 supplies no configuration key, so the server whose configuration changed is the identity — server_host is that field, not decoration.
