## Notes

MERGED: the inherited candidate ReplacementAddressAssociatedWithMailbox (§7.7) names the identical state change — a mailbox acquires a replacement address — and its name is carried here rather than kept, because §7.7's phrasing is neutral over both of §3.4's two cases while this event's fields fit the "new address" case the section actually describes. Its server_host field was dropped as it belongs to the configuration root, not to the relocation fact. Rejected alternative names from the merge: ReplacementAddressAssociatedWithMailbox, UserRelocated (the name the §4.2-4.3 sweep recorded as a cut candidate in extras.md, "the unwritten precondition of 251/551"), AddressChangeKnown. Strength: no RFC 2119 keyword anywhere — §3.4 describes the need, §7.7 worries only about disclosing the association; both presuppose an event neither specifies. Marked likely for exactly that reason. Real contrast available in the RFC's own data: Appendix D.1 line 4898 answers 550 No such user here for \<Green@foo.com>; a site holding this record would have answered 551 and named the replacement, which is precisely the disclosure §7.7 flags. Screens: altitude tiers, inbound-only scope, responsibility-boundary cutoff. Citation fixed: the inherited §7.7 ref quoted a bare mid-sentence line fragment (line 4335 alone) under the label 4334-4336; extended here to the whole sentence, which does span 4334-4336.

⚠️ FIELD HYGIENE PASS (fields and notes only): struck one field, effective_from (Tue, 19 May 1998 09:14:07 -0700). Two things are wrong with it. The value is the walk scene's timestamp, not the RFC's — RFC 5321's own example dates are Thu, 21 May 1998 (rfc5321.txt:5014, 5064) — and the field itself has no source at all: §3.4 states no start, no end and no lifetime for a forwarding entry, which the notes above already record as a silence, and an event's own occurrence time is not payload in any case. Nothing was added to replace it: the spec names no key for a relocation record, and identity is carried by prior_mailbox, the address the relocation is a fact about. Sourced: prior_mailbox at rfc5321.txt:4897; replacement_mailbox is composed — Green is the RFC's own user with no mailbox at foo.com (4884) and xyz.com is the RFC's own relay host (1940), but the address \<Green@xyz.com> appears nowhere in RFC 5321, and that is stated plainly rather than dressed as a citation.
