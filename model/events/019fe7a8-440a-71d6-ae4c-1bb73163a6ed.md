## Notes

RECOVERED BY THE RE-SWEEP; 7.1 contributed nothing to the 352 inherited candidates, the whole spoofing subsection lost to the 32-event cap. Strength: no RFC 2119 keyword anywhere in 7.1 — the capability is stated as an inherent property, neither permitted nor forbidden. Rejected names: SpoofedMessageCreated, ForgedMailInjected, MessageForged, NaiveRecipientTricked. No opposite arm exists in the RFC: no transport-level detection is specified and 4167-4169 says why, the section's most consequential absence. Sibling: SpoofedOriginDetected. Screens that would have killed it: inbound-only scope, the capture/decision lens, the responsibility-boundary cutoff. FIELD HYGIENE: struck receiving_host (foo.com) as padding — it restates the actor's own side of the connection and 7.1 asks nothing of it; the two survivors are the lie and the truth. No identity key was fabricated here, and none exists to fabricate: RFC 5321 names no session, transaction or connection identifier, so while the session lives the channel is its identity. actual_client_address stays because the address the server derives from the TCP connection is the one peer identity the spec acknowledges (4.4 lines 3325-3327).
