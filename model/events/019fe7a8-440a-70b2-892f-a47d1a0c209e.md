A server refused a message because of perceived defects in its header section or body, which the specification tells servers not to do.

## Where RFC 5321 says so

| Section | Lines | Text |
|:--|:--|:--|
| §3.3 | 1152-1158 | *"When the RFC 822 format ([28], [4]) is being used, the mail data include the header fields such as those named Date, Subject, To, Cc, and From. Server SMTP systems SHOULD NOT reject messages based on perceived defects in the RFC 822 or MIME (RFC 2045 [21]) message header section or message body. In particular, they MUST NOT reject messages in which the numbers of Resent-header fields do not match or Resent-to appears without Resent-from and/or Resent-date."* |
| §4.5.3.1.8 | 3540-3543 | *"The general principle that relaying SMTP server MUST NOT, and delivery SMTP servers SHOULD NOT, perform validation tests on message header fields suggests that messages SHOULD NOT be rejected based on the total number of recipients shown in header fields."* |

Line numbers are into [`docs/rfc/rfc5321.txt`](../../docs/rfc/rfc5321.txt), the archived copy. Quotations keep the specification's own words and spellings (AGENTS.md rule 2).

## Notes

RECOVERED BY THE RE-SWEEP. The sweeps folded only the HONORED arm into MessageAcceptedForProcessing's notes, as "MessageAcceptedDespiteHeaderDefects"; the violated arm — which is what actually happens on the Internet — had no event. Strength is two-tier in one sentence and that is the point: SHOULD NOT for defects in general, MUST NOT for the specific Resent-header cases, so one event covers a discouraged act and a forbidden one and the `defect` field is what tells them apart. The §4.5.3.1.8 citation, added by re-sweep, generalizes it and adds the role distinction: a RELAY must not even inspect header fields, a DELIVERY server should not. This is the same prohibition the Relaying chapter meets from the other side as MessageDataModifiedByRelay; cross-referenced, not claimed. Opposite event: MessageAcceptedForProcessing, which is the honored arm and needs no separate name. Screens: the twelve-slice model has no content inspection at all, so both arms are invisible to it. Rejected alternative names: MessageRejectedForMalformedHeaderSection, HeaderValidationRejectionApplied.

FIELD HYGIENE PASS: struck two of three. transaction_id is fabricated — RFC 5321 supplies no transaction key; identity is the channel and, for the refused message, the forward paths the transaction accumulated. reply_code is struck as a rendering — no line of RFC 5321 attaches a code to this prohibition at all, so 550 was doubly unsupported. defect survives and is the strongest-sourced field in the chapter: "Resent-to appears without Resent-from" is the RFC's own wording at rfc5321.txt:1158, and it is the field that tells the sentence's MUST NOT tier from its SHOULD NOT tier, exactly as the note above argues.
