## Notes

Harvested unchanged. The condition is exact and easy to get wrong: the period is deleted ONLY when there are other characters on the line — a lone period is the terminator and is never unstuffed, which is why EndOfMailIndicatorRecognized and this event come from the same three lines and are still two events. This is the point at which the content the model believes it accepted stops being byte-identical to what arrived: any content hash taken before unstuffing differs from one taken after, and the project's content_ref field (blob:sha256, seen in docs/paths/EXPLORE-rewalk-cadence.md line 222) does not say which side of this event it is computed on — a live hotspot, not a hypothetical. Strength: none, same as its inverse. Screens: same as DotStuffingApplied. Rejected alternative names: TransparencyEncodingRemoved, LeadingPeriodStripped.

FIELD HYGIENE PASS: struck one of three. transaction_id is fabricated — RFC 5321 supplies no transaction key; identity is the channel and the line's position in the content stream. Both surviving fields stay: they are the same line on either side of the transform, which is what makes the reversibility checkable and what makes the content_ref hotspot above statable at all. Values illustrative and labeled so; §4.5.2 gives the rule and never an instance.
