## Notes

CITATION FIX: the inherited candidate attributed the reply text 'S: 550 Access Denied to You.' to §4.1.1.3 line 1323. The LINE is right and the SECTION is wrong — 1323 sits inside §3.5.1 (heading at 1215; §3.5.2 begins at 1335), a VRFY example. Corrected here after opening the file; exactly the failure mode rule 3 exists for. MAY strength — a server is permitted to refuse and equally permitted not to, and the RFC then expresses a preference for the other route (see SourceRouteStripped, where MAY-ignore is paired with SHOULD-do-so). ⚠️ AMBIGUITY WORTH FLAGGING: §3.6.1 specifies NO code for this refusal. The 550 SHOULD elsewhere in §3.6 attaches to policy refusal of RELAYING, not to refusal of a source-routed address, and it would be easy to over-read one into the other. Kept separate from SourceRouteStripped because a refusal is not an acceptance, and separate from RelayingDeclined because the ground differs — the route syntax versus the destination. Screens: nothing kills it outright, but the twelve-slice model has no source-route slice.
