## Notes

RECOVERED BY THE RE-SWEEP. Named as a sibling in idx 298's and idx 292's notes and never given a row of its own — §7.6 is a nine-line section and the sweep that owned it spent its cap elsewhere. Strength is deliberately weak and easy to over-report: §7.6 uses a lowercase 'should', not a 2119 SHOULD, so this is advice rather than a requirement, and a server that discloses is fully conforming. That is the finding, not a footnote. The harm is delayed and lands on a party who is not in the session: the disclosure happens at stamping time, is discovered whenever the message is read, and the injured recipient never appears in any SMTP dialogue. Cause events: TraceRecipientNarrowed (picking the wrong single path), MultiplePathsStampedInForClause. Prevention: TraceForClauseOmitted.
