## Notes

RECOVERED BY THE RE-SWEEP. The inherited set carried the DEFENSES 7.9 prescribes and not the attack that motivates them, yet the sentence at 4375-4377 is the only statement in the RFC of what open relaying is actually for. Doubtful for two honest reasons, both recorded rather than resolved: the abused relay cannot observe the hostile INTENT, only the relaying; and the actual origin is by construction the thing being hidden. Strength: none - descriptive history, no keyword. Sibling: RelayAbuseDetected, the observer's counterpart, not given a slot because 7.9 does not describe detection. Rejected names: OpenRelayAbused, MailOriginConcealedByRelay, RelayHijacked. Screens: all of them. --- FIELD HYGIENE: struck actual_origin on the inherited note's own reasoning - the origin is by construction hidden, so the field is unfillable at the moment the event occurs, and a value reconstructed later from trace analysis is a read model's output, not this event's payload. Struck claimed_domain: 7.9 has no basis for it at all, an EHLO claim belongs to the session chapter. The relay itself survives because here it is the counterparty, not the recorder - the actor is the hostile client - which is the test that decides every other host field in this chapter.
