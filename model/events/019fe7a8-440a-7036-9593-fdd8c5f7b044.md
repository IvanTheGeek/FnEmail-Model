# `ObsoleteDateFormUsedInTraceLine`

| Section | Lines | Text |

| §F.5 | 5225-5227 | *"Two-digit years are deprecated; three-digit years were never permitted in the Internet mail system."* |

## Notes

RECOVERED BY THE RE-SWEEP. idx 195's notes spotted the prohibition and called it 'a MUST NOT hiding inside a comment in the grammar, easy to miss' — and then did not give it an event, which is exactly the loss the cap causes. It is worth its own row because the strength is a full MUST NOT while everything else in the date cluster is SHOULD, so folding it into TraceTimeFormatConfigured would bury a mandatory rule inside three advisory ones. Rule 2 applied literally: F.5 line 5225 reads 'four-digit years MUST BE used' with that capitalization, which is not RFC 2119's spelling and is copied here as it stands rather than normalized to 'MUST'. The prohibition is stated twice, in two places a reader is unlikely to visit together — an ABNF comment at 3314-3315 and a Deprecated Features appendix at 5222-5227 — and neither cross-references the other. Sibling: TraceDateStampedWithZoneNameInsteadOfOffset. FnEmail screens: the responsibility-boundary cutoff and the inbound-only scope, since the offending stamp is always upstream; the altitude tiers would call it Protocol. ⚠️ FIELD HYGIENE PASS: struck the fabricated `message_ref` identity key — the RFC names none, and f2C8D14 is not in it. The malformed date is the identity and the violation together. Sourcing recorded: the value is the walk scene's timestamp with the year truncated, which is precisely the 'especially two-digit years' case the MUST NOT at 3314-3315 names, so the example instantiates the prohibited form rather than gesturing at it.
