## Notes

Carried from #82 and SPLIT: the inherited candidate carried a verified Boolean, which packs two opposite outcomes into one event; the negative verdict is now ReturnPathVerificationFailed, per the rule that a refusal is not an acceptance. Sits right on the boundary the 'guest viewed calendar' test polices - the checking is a read, the VERDICT once recorded and relied upon is a fact later behavior depends on, because the server will or will not send a notification to that address because of it. Flagged rather than resolved, which is what Step 1 asks. Strength: MAY, and unusually weak even for a MAY - the RFC declines to name a method AND declines to recommend one, so two conforming servers can reach opposite verdicts on the same path. HOTSPOT, not a detail. SPF [29] and DKIM [30] [31] are named at 1467-1468 as 'Recent work', which dates the text: RFC 5321 is October 2008. Rejected names: ReturnPathValidated, ReversePathChecked. --- FIELD HYGIENE: struck verified_at (event time restated). method_name survives with a real source at 1468, and it is a fact rather than a default precisely because the RFC declines to pick a method. The address is re-based on Appendix D.3.
