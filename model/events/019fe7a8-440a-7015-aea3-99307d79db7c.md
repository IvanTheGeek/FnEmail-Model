## Notes

Carried from the inherited candidate. Strength: a MAY for the limit's existence against a SHOULD floor of two addresses three lines later, so a configured limit of 1 is simultaneously permitted and discouraged and the two clauses openly conflict: 🟥. The RFC also records the disagreement itself at 3900-3902 — an unusual thing for a spec to admit, and worth keeping because it explains why the rule is weak. alternates_enabled carries the 'or disable' half of 3899-3900 and is not padding: disabled and limit-of-1 are different configurations. Consequences carried separately: AlternateAddressLimitReached and DeliveryAbandonedAfterSingleAddress. Rejected alternative names: AlternateAddressUseDisabled (only half the event), MultihomedRetryPolicySet. Which project screen would have killed it: the altitude tiers place configuration outside the protocol tier, and the consumer test has nothing to consume it. ⚠️ FIELD HYGIENE PASS: nothing struck; no fabricated identity key was present. alternates_enabled is sourced to the RFC's own 'limit or disable' at 3899 and the inherited defense of it stands. scope is the identity of the configuration and is flagged as invented framing — RFC 5321 has no notion of a configuration scope, so the value is illustrative and a later step may find the installation belongs in the actor slot instead. The limit value is illustrative because the RFC states no number, only that a limit MAY exist and that at least two addresses SHOULD be tried.
