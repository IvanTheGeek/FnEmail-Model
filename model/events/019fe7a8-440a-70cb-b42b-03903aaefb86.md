## Notes

MAY strength. The clearest case in the chapter where a deliberately THIN payload is the fact rather than a padding failure: the meaningful content is the ABSENCE of the forwarding address, and this event must never be padded with the suppressed address — that would invert its meaning. The kit's two statements meet here and the tension is left standing rather than resolved: fieldless or near-fieldless events are legitimate, and an event without fields is an opaque label. Contrast with RecipientRejectedWithForwardingAddress, which carries the address the operator chose to disclose; the two differ only in what is NOT said, which is why they must not be merged into one event with an optional field. Screens: 'one event per command', and the capture/decision two-kinds lens, in which a refusal is neither a capture nor a decision.
