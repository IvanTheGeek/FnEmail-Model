## Notes

RECOVERED BY THE RE-SWEEP, and the single most consequential omission the cap caused. The inherited set carried the honored arm of the strongest MUST in the chapter and not the violated one, yet the violated arm is the failure mode users actually experience: mail accepted, never delivered, never bounced. Strength: MUST, with no exception clause - a server that does this is non-conforming, which is why the event exists and why it is worth recording. Distinct from NotificationSuppressedToPreventLoop: that suppression is MANDATORY (a MUST NOT), this discard is FORBIDDEN, and they look identical from outside - same silence, opposite conformance. Keeping both, cross-referenced, is the point. Rejected names: MailBlackholed, NotificationObligationBreached, SilentDropOccurred. --- FIELD HYGIENE: struck relay_host (recorder's own domain). queue_id kept on the 4.4 ID clause, the stream root being Queue Entry. The two addresses are the facts that make the violation legible - what was owed and who was never told - and both are now sourced to Appendix D.3 rather than to invented hosts.
