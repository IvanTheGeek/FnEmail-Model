## Notes

RECOVERED BY THE RE-SWEEP out of the set-aside pile, where an earlier sweep named it and correctly refused to keep the comparison itself: comparing Received fields is a read across messages, the classic 'guest viewed calendar' case. The *finding* is different — once recorded it is a fact an operator acts on. Kept, doubtful, with the boundary named rather than hidden, which is what Step 1 asks. Deliberately no elapsed-time field: an interval computed from two timestamps is exactly the derived value the kit sends to a read model, and both endpoints are here for anyone who wants it. STREAM ROOT INVENTED: 'Relay Host'. None of the offered roots fits — the fact is about a third-party host observed from outside, not about the Session, the Message, or a Next-Hop Destination this system will ever connect to. Preconditions that make it possible: TraceLinePrepended at every hop, ExistingTraceLinePreserved throughout, and offsets rather than zone names (TraceDateStampedWithZoneNameInsteadOfOffset is what breaks it). This is the one purpose §4.4 states for trace data other than loop detection. FnEmail screens: every altitude tier, the consumer test, and the responsibility-boundary cutoff. ⚠️ FIELD HYGIENE PASS: nothing struck. This event never carried `message_ref`, which is right — it is about a host, not a message, and the earlier refusal to add an elapsed-time field anticipated rule 4 of this pass exactly: an interval derived from two stamps is a read-model value, and both endpoints are kept instead. Sourcing is now stated and two of the three values are honestly invented — mx.bar.com is not in the RFC, and the egress stamp is the walk timestamp moved forward to make the delay visible per rule 8; only the ingress stamp comes from the walk scene.
