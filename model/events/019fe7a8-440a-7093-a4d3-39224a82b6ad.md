## Notes

Merged ConnectionClosedDefensively (§3.8/§7.8 sweep) and ConnectionClosedForAbuse (Appendix/security sweep). CITATION ERROR FOUND AND FIXED: the ConnectionClosedForAbuse candidate cited §7.8 lines 4351-4353 for a quote that sits on line 4352 alone; the full sentence runs 4349-4353 and is cited that way here. Rejected alternative names: ConnectionClosedForAbuse, AbusiveConnectionDropped, DefensiveDisconnect. Kept apart from an abuse-detection event for the same reason ServiceShutdownDetected is kept apart from ServiceUnavailableAnnounced: the judgment and the act are distinct, and a server may detect abuse and choose to tarpit rather than close. The asymmetry is the real find — the client gets 5yz replies and then silence, with no 221 and no 421, so from the client's side this is indistinguishable from ConnectionLostWithoutQuit, and that indistinguishability is a modeling fact rather than an accident. 🟥 Hotspot: 'an appropriate number' is left wholly to the implementer. replies_issued_before_close is recorded at the moment of the decision, not computed at read time. Screens: the one-event-per-command target — this event is caused by an accumulation of commands, so no single command owns it — and the whole abuse lane is absent from the current twelve slices even though it is inbound. FIELD HYGIENE PASS: struck 4 fields, event is now FIELDLESS, and the justification is untouched — §7.8 licenses the close and the asymmetry finding lives in the prose. session_id struck: no such key in the spec. peer_address struck as the channel's, recorded at TransmissionChannelOpened; if an abuse lane is ever built the attacker's address is a stream root, not payload here. final_reply_code 550 struck as a rendering, and §7.8 line 4352 does not even fix it — '5yz (normally 550)'. ⚠️ replies_issued_before_close struck, against the note above: a count of prior replies is a fold over events already recorded, which is the definition of a read model, and the RFC supplies no number at all since 'an appropriate number' is left to the implementer. The hotspot that indeterminacy creates is preserved above.
