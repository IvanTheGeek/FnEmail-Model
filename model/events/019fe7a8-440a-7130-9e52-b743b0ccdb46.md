## Notes

The violated arm of a SHOULD written as prose: "SHOULD simply utilize" plus "strongly discouraged" together amount to a SHOULD NOT the RFC never spells with the keyword. Because it is discouraged and not forbidden, implementations do it, and when they do the sender silently never sees their own post — that silence is precisely why it deserves an event, since without one the missing delivery has no record anywhere. suppressed_address is the sender's own address in the common case, which is why Smith@bar.com is used: the same address that is the reverse-path in Appendix D.1 line 4893. Rejected alternative names: OriginatorEliminatedFromExpansion, RecipientFilteredFromList, ExpansionAddressDropped. Sibling with a different actor and a different motive: DuplicateRecipientEliminatedBySourceExpansion (§3.5.4 1410-1417), which is the same suppression performed at the originating end and deprecated by a real SHOULD NOT. The honored arm — every address on the list used — produces no event and is recorded in set_aside as a read. Screens: the consumer test G1 kills it outright, since nothing in the model consumes it. FIELD HYGIENE PASS: nothing struck. The `rule` enum was tested against the source and kept rather than trimmed, because the RFC's own wording is open — "heuristics or other matching rules" (1704-1705) — so an open value set is what the text supports; only originator_match traces to words on the page ("such as that of the originator", 1705-1706) and the field's source now says so, which is the honest form of a partly-illustrative enum. It is not a status: it names the rule that fired at the moment it fired, and is not recoverable from any other event. queue_id kept on the §4.4 ID-clause warrant.
