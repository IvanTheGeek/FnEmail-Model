## Notes

Recovered by the re-sweep as the sibling arm of the MAY at 2962-2963 that MessageRequeued takes; the inherited set carried a MessageHeldForUserReview under a different chapter's span but nothing tied it to the queue decision. Distinct from DeliveryGivenUp and from any bounce: this is a local hand-back to the submitting user, not a notification mailed to the envelope reverse-path, and blurring the two loses the fact that no mail was generated. Rejected names: MessageHeldForUserReview, MessageReturnedToSender. The notification-generating arm belongs to the bounce chapter and is cross-referenced, not claimed. Screens: inbound-only scope, responsibility-boundary cutoff. ⚠️ FIELD HYGIENE PASS: STRUCK declining_reply_code on the same ground as MessageRequeued's — the 4yz class is the trigger for the decision at 2961-2964, and the code is not data the RFC carries beyond the reply. returned_to is kept as the one fact that makes the event mean anything, with the caveat that the RFC says only "the user" (2961-2962) and supplies no identifier for a local submitter; the walk-scene mailbox stands in, and a real implementation's local user handle may not be a mailbox at all. queue_entry_id kept on §4.4's ID-clause ground (rfc5321.txt:3336); f2C8D14 is this project's minted value, not the RFC's.
