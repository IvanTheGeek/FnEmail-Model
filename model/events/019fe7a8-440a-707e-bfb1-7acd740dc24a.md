## Notes

Kept DISTINCT from ServiceExtensionsAdvertised, which the raw set nearly collapsed: advertising is the offer, this is the offer-and-acceptance pair, and the RFC keeps them apart in its own words — 'offered by the server and accepted (requested) by the client'. No other event in the chapter has this reach: it is cited four separate times as the thing that lifts otherwise-absolute rules — the 2-to-5 first-digit MUST NOT, the lock-step MUST wait, the sequencing table, and the no-parameters MUST NOT on RSET/DATA/QUIT. Folded siblings recorded rather than carried: ClientBlockedAwaitingReply, the lock-step wait state this event lifts, which is worth its own event if pipelining is ever modeled since the wait is the default; EnhancedStatusCodeAdopted (§4.3.2 lines 3051-3054, RFC 3463 SHOULD); and EightBitTransmissionEnabledForSession, cut for the cap, which names the specific rule 8BITMIME lifts — Appendix A lines 4715-4718 widen 7-bit to full 8-bit 'as part of the message body, or, if specifically designed to do so, in SMTP commands or responses', two scopes, and the appendix never says at what moment the rule changes, so whether the offer, the acceptance or first use is the state change is a genuine 🟥 hotspot. Screens: the HELO-only charter kills this whole family by charter, not by evidence. FIELD HYGIENE PASS: struck 2 fields. session_id struck — no such key; identity is the channel whose rules are being changed. extensions_offered struck as a duplicate: the offer is already recorded, in full, as ehlo_keywords on ServiceExtensionsAdvertised, and copying it here is the read-model fold the method's field rules push out of events. extensions_accepted survives as the new fact, and it is the half the RFC's own wording at lines 3049-3051 attributes to the client.
