## Notes

Recovered by the re-sweep; no sweep carried this sentence at all. Doubtful, and honestly so: "may be further modified" carries no keyword, names no modification, and points forward to §5.1's multihomed rules rather than specifying anything itself — the whole sentence is a pointer. Kept because Step 1 is generative and because a strategy change scoped to a destination is a genuine state change even when its content is unspecified, and because dropping it would leave the only bridge between this chapter's per-destination backoff and the address-resolution chapter's per-address attempts unrecorded. host_addresses is the one genuinely List-valued field here besides the recipient paths. Screens: inbound-only scope, altitude tiers, and the consumer test G1, which nothing here survives. ⚠️ FIELD HYGIENE PASS: NOTHING STRUCK. host_addresses survives the address-resolution objection that struck host_address from HostMarkedUnreachable and HostReachabilityRestored, because here the RFC's own sentence is ABOUT the multiplicity of addresses (3732-3734) — the plurality is the trigger, not an imported lookup result. No timestamp and no session, transaction or connection key: RFC 5321 supplies none, and the destination host carries identity. Confidence stays doubtful on its original ground, that the sentence specifies nothing; note that nothing on the payload says what the strategy became, which is the same gap seen from the field side.
