## Notes

MUST on the code: 452 for an exhausted IMPLEMENTATION limit. A recipient count was considered as a field and DROPPED as computed. Kept strictly separate from RecipientLimitEnforcedBySitePolicy: same trigger shape, opposite semantics — capacity exhaustion is transient and MUST be 452, policy refusal is permanent and MAY be 5yz, and the client's retry behavior forks on exactly that, so collapsing them into one event with a code field would erase the fork. Two siblings that were folded into this candidate's notes are now their own events, as the candidate itself asked: OverflowReplyReclassifiedAsTransient (the client's 552 handling) and PreviouslyAcceptedRecipientsSilentlyDiscarded (the forbidden arm, which the sweep called 'the clearest both-arms pair in the span' and said 'was cut only for slot budget and should be restored').
