## Notes

The word 'always' is doing the work: an occasional 554 does not qualify, so this is a standing configuration and not a per-connection outcome. It has a consequence outside its own stream — it is the only arrangement that lifts the MUST to support the reserved postmaster mailbox — which is what makes it worth an event rather than a setting. Complement not carried, cut for the cap: ConnectionRefusalLifted. Status-shaped on purpose: this puts the whole server into a state, and the condemned status/phase register would kill it outright, as would the consumer test; the brief rules both out of Step 1, and the corpus authors use status-shaped events in their own worked material. Session-level sibling it causes on every connection: MailSessionRefusedAtConnectionOpening.
