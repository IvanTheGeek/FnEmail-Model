# `SourceRouteNamesCopiedIntoReversePath`

## Notes

RECOVERED BY THE RE-SWEEP: the VIOLATED ARM of a MUST NOT that rides along inside a sentence every sweep cited for its OTHER half. Two sweeps quoted line 1930-1931 in full while modeling only the stripping; the prohibition on copying names into the reverse-path is a separate rule about a separate buffer and had no event at all. It constrains what a relay may put in its own MAIL FROM, which is why it belongs to this chapter and not to a routing one. Values are built from the RFC's own example at 1943-1950 — the relay is xyz.com and the original reverse-path is \<userx@y.foo.org>. conforming_reverse_path is included so the violation is legible without re-deriving it; it is a stated fact of the same event, not a computation over the stream. Kept as a distinct event from SourceRouteStripped: stripping the forward-path and polluting the reverse-path are opposite behaviors and one MUST NOT does not follow from one SHOULD. Screens: inbound-only scope, the responsibility-boundary cutoff, and the twelve-slice model, which has no relay path. Confidence 'likely' because the RFC states the prohibition and never describes the violation, so the event's shape is inferred from the rule.

FIELD HYGIENE PASS: struck two fields. relay_host (xyz.com) goes as ambient — the relay is the actor writing this reverse-path, so its identity belongs to the session it is opening, not to a payload field; xyz.com is genuinely the RFC's relay at 1940, which is why the value looked sourced while the field was still padding. conforming_reverse_path struck as COMPUTED. ⚠️ The note above defends it explicitly — 'included so the violation is legible without re-deriving it; it is a stated fact of the same event, not a computation over the stream' — and the defense concedes the point in its own words: not a computation over the stream, but a computation over the other field on this same event, obtained by deleting the at-domain names that the MUST NOT forbids copying. The method's rule is about computed values, wherever they are computed from, and the derivation here is the prohibition itself read backwards. reverse_path_written survives and is the violation; its example is labeled illustrative because RFC 5321 never shows the non-conforming form, though both halves of the string are its own.
