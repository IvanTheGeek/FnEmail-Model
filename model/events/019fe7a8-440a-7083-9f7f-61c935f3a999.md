## Notes

Kept apart from BufferCleared, not merged into it, because §2.3.6 names two separable devices — a buffer holding contents and a state table holding position — and offers them as two things a client may do. The complication, preserved rather than resolved: the same sentence attributes BOTH consequences (buffer discarded AND state returned) to the state-table reset, so the two may not be cleanly separable, and treating them as one event is a modeling choice §2.3.6 does not license either way. Deliberate ambiguity in the source, also preserved: 'returned to some previous state' never says which previous state, and §2.3.6 does not say the session returns to its start. §4.1.1.1 lines 1822-1825 settle it for the greeting case only — no transaction in progress, all state tables and buffers cleared — and say nothing about the identity claim, which survives a reset in practice. Do not fill that in from memory.
