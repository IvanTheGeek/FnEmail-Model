## Notes

Recovered by the re-sweep, and INVENTED rather than cited: the RFC states no removal rule for the unreachable list anywhere. Doubtful for that reason, and kept because without it the list grows without bound and every destination eventually becomes permanently unreachable — the spec has a write with no matching erase. This is a hotspot under rule 12, to be marked wherever the unreachable-list steps get drawn. The one hint the RFC gives is 3725-3727, where inbound contact from a host is evidence its mail can now be sent, which is also what QueueFlushTriggeredByPeerContact records — whether those are one event or two is unresolved and left that way. Screens: inbound-only scope, condemned status register. ⚠️ FIELD HYGIENE PASS: STRUCK host_address for the same reason as on HostMarkedUnreachable — the list the RFC describes at 3716-3718 is a list of hosts, not addresses. STRUCK restored_at as a bare occurrence stamp. What is left is the host alone, which is the identity key and, correctly, the whole of what this event can say: the confidence stays doubtful on its original ground, that the RFC states no removal rule, and the thin payload is a second expression of the same gap rather than a new problem.
