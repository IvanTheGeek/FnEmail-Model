## Notes

RECOVERED BY THE RE-SWEEP. Sweep #302 named it in its notes as a 'Sibling configuration event' and had no slot - the cap again. This is the anti-open-relay configuration, and 7.9 is the only place in the RFC that states the rationale: relaying through arbitrary sites is used to hide the origins of mail (4375-4377). Strength: TWO different things in one sentence, not to be flattened - 'Some sites have decided' is descriptive of practice with no keyword at all, while 'implementations SHOULD provide the capability to perform this type of filtering' is a SHOULD laid on the IMPLEMENTOR, not on the operator. So there is a third, separable event this chapter deliberately does not spend a slot on: RelayFilteringCapabilityProvided, actor a software vendor, cited to the same lines. 4.1.4 lines 2498-2500 supply the basis the restriction runs on, which implies a ClientAuthenticated event upstream that no section of this chapter names. Distinct from RelayPolicyConfigured and SelectiveAcceptancePolicyAdopted. Rejected names: OpenRelayClosed, RelayFilteringEnabled. --- FIELD HYGIENE: struck server_host (recorder's own domain) and restricted_at (event time restated). restriction_basis survives and is now sourced to the RFC's own words at 2499, 'based on the client's IP address'.
