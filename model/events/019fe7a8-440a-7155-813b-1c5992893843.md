## Notes

RECOVERED BY THE RE-SWEEP. Named as an over-time sibling in the raw candidate ReturnAddressDeterminedInvalid's notes and given no slot — the cap. It belongs in the chapter because §6.2 4052-4053 speaks of the policy "being adopted" as a discrete act with consequences that outlast any session: a configuration event with a lifetime measured in years, of exactly the kind the brainstorming step is meant to surface and that session-shaped models never reach. The SHOULD attaches to this event, not to the drop: it constrains how the adopted policy is applied, so certainty_threshold is part of the policy as adopted rather than of each drop. No revocation event is offered because the RFC states no lifetime for such a policy; a future reader should not take that silence for a ruling. Rejected alternative names: DropPolicyForInvalidReturnAddressesConfigured, InvalidSenderPolicyEnabled, ReturnAddressValidationPolicySet. Which screens would have killed it: all of them, honestly — no command, no session, no inbound traffic, no consumer, and the four altitude tiers would file it as Product tier rather than Protocol.
