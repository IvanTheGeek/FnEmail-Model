## Notes

RECOVERED BY THE RE-SWEEP as the opposite arm of ListReclassifiedAsFullMua — the inherited set carried the modifying arm (through MessageModifiedByList, DeliveryAcceptedByList, MessagePostedByList) and nothing at all for the constrained arm, which is the one that keeps the message identity intact across the hop. Deliberately near-fieldless: identity plus the message it applied to, and nothing else varies. The kit permits this ("If an event has no meaningful payload beyond its identity ... it is fine to have no fields or just the identity key") and its own counter-warning applies at the same time ("An event without fields is an opaque label") — recorded, not resolved, per the prompt. Marked doubtful for a second reason, honestly stated: "can be treated as" is a permission to classify, not a report that anything changed, so this may be a read model over MessageModifiedByList's absence rather than an event. The §3.9 sweep set exactly that judgment aside as a fold; the chapter charter names the classification explicitly, so both arms are on the table for a facilitator to cut. Rejected alternative names: ListEmulatedMta, ListRemainedInTransit, ConstrainedListProcessed. FIELD HYGIENE PASS: queue_id struck — 1 field. The §4.4 ID-clause warrant does not reach it, because the RFC frames this classification as a property of the list and not of a message: the subject of the sentence at 1730-1733 is "such lists", and the verb is "can be treated as". Attaching a per-message queue id asserted a per-message classification the sentence does not support, and it also made the record look like a Message-stream event when its stream_root is List. What is left is the pseudo-mailbox, which is the List stream's own identity — exactly the "just the identity key" case the method sanctions, and the shape the earlier note said the event substantively has. Confidence left at doubtful: the strike does not weaken the candidate, it clarifies it, and the fold objection recorded above is unchanged.
