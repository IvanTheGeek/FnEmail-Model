## Notes

RECOVERED BY THE RE-SWEEP as its own event; the inherited set had only TraceRecipientNarrowed (4.4), the narrowing MUST, which named this disclosure in its notes as 'BlindCopyRecipientDisclosedInTraceField' without promoting it. Strength: a LOWERCASE 'should be supplied with caution or not at all' (4327-4328), flagged in the 7.x span notes as one of the section's misleading lowercase shoulds. The honored arm considered and folded: ForClauseOmittedToProtectRecipients, from 'or not at all' (4328) — omitting an optional clause is arguably nothing happening and is indistinguishable in the trace line from a server that never supports FOR; recorded so a facilitator can promote it. The RFC's cross-reference chain closes on itself: 4.4 lines 3175-3176 point to 7.2, 7.2 lines 4219-4223 forbid copying RCPT arguments into trace header fields, and 7.6 points back at the FOR clause. Rejected names: ForClauseLeakedBlindCopy, TraceRecipientDisclosed, BlindCopyIdentityExposedInTrace. Screens that would have killed it: the 'one event per command' target. FIELD HYGIENE: struck stamping_host, an actor restatement. The survivors are the disclosure itself — who the FOR clause names and who else can therefore read it — which is what 4327-4330 warns about, with 4.4 lines 3173-3176 confirming exactly one path may appear however many RCPT commands were given. No identity key: the trace line belongs to a message, and RFC 5321 gives a message none.
