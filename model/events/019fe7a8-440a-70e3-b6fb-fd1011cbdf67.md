## Notes

Recovered by the re-sweep as the violated arm of the MUST at 3703-3704; the honored arm is RetryScheduled and no sweep carried the violation. The event covers two different transgressions at two different strengths and this is deliberate: zero delay violates a MUST, while a delay under thirty minutes only falls short of a SHOULD, and elapsed_since_failure_minutes is the field that tells them apart from the payload alone. A facilitator may reasonably want these split into two events; recording the tension rather than resolving it. Rejected name: RetryIntervalViolated. Screens: inbound-only scope; the condemned status register would also object, since this is a compliance verdict shaped like a fact. ⚠️ FIELD HYGIENE PASS — CONFIDENCE LOWERED TO DOUBTFUL: STRUCK elapsed_since_failure_minutes, which is this event's timestamp minus DeliveryAttemptFailed.attempted_at — a computed value, and the method sends computed values to read models. The paragraph above is left standing because it is the record of what the event was built to do, but it no longer describes what the event can do: with the computed field gone, the payload is identical to a conforming retry's, the MUST violation and the SHOULD shortfall are indistinguishable, and the whole verdict is only visible by folding this event's position against the preceding failure. That fold is a read model, so what is left may be a comparison wearing an event's name. Kept and marked doubtful rather than dropped; deciding is a later step's job. queue_entry_id kept on §4.4's ID-clause ground (rfc5321.txt:3336), destination_host is the Next-Hop Destination identity the MUST at 3703-3704 is scoped to.
