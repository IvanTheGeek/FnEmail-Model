## Notes

Recovered by the re-sweep, and INVENTED rather than cited: the RFC gives no invalidation rule beyond "great care". Doubtful for that reason, and kept for the same structural argument as HostReachabilityRestored — a cache with a write and no erase is not a cache, and the spec's own observation that answers vary within a single connection (3747-3749) is an argument that entries must expire fast. A hotspot under rule 12. Rejected name: NegativeResponseCacheExpired. Screens: inbound-only scope, altitude tiers. ⚠️ FIELD HYGIENE PASS: STRUCK invalidated_at as a bare occurrence stamp; since the RFC states no cache lifetime, no rule measures from it. server_host and command_verb together are the cache key, so they are the identity of what was erased rather than payload padding; EHLO is the RFC's own example of a command whose answers vary at 3747-3748. Confidence stays doubtful on its original ground.
