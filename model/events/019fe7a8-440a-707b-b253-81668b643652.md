## Notes

RECOVERED BY THE RE-SWEEP. No inherited candidate touched lines 1802-1807 at all, even though three separate sweeps cited §4.1.1.1. This is the violated arm of a SHOULD NOT that the RFC simultaneously concedes will happen and asks servers to tolerate — both arms are real: a client sends it (this event) and a server either tolerates it or refuses the greeting for it, the latter landing in GreetingRejected. The advice to servers is not 2119 language at all — 'it is probably wise' — which is weaker than a SHOULD and is exactly the kind of prose a keyword-hunting sweep skips. Rejected alternative names: TrailingIdentificationStringSent, LegacyGreetingDecorationSent. Screens: the responsibility-boundary cutoff and the consumer test — nothing consumes a tolerated stray token, which is the point of tolerating it. FIELD HYGIENE PASS: struck 1 field. session_id struck — no such key in the spec; identity here is the greeting the string trails, so address_literal carries it, which is why that field stays even though the substitution itself is a separate event. trailing_identification has no RFC example — the RFC describes the convention and shows no instance of it — so its value is marked illustrative rather than sourced; it survives because it is the whole content of the event and its absence would leave nothing to distinguish this from a plain address-literal greeting.
