## Notes

RECOVERED BY THE RE-SWEEP. 7.9's first paragraph had only its per-message consequence in the inherited set (MailRejectedForPolicyReason); the standing decision that produces it was missing. The RFC's own phrase 'if a site decides to be selective' (4363-4365) names the decision as an act. Strength: the permission is unconditional and pointedly non-normative - 'a well-established principle that an SMTP server may refuse to accept mail for any operational or technical reason that makes sense to the site' - while the restraint is a LOWERCASE 'should be taken', not the RFC 2119 keyword, verified by opening the file. So the balance 7.9 asks for is advice, not a requirement, and there is no violated arm with normative force; the nearest thing is a site rejecting so much traffic that ubiquity 'will be threatened' - recorded as a sibling, ExcessiveRejectionPracticed, without a slot because nothing observable marks the threshold. Broader than RelayPolicyConfigured. Rejected names: TrafficSelectivityAdopted, AcceptancePolicyNarrowed. --- FIELD HYGIENE: struck site_host (recorder's own domain) and adopted_at (event time restated). selection_ground survives and quotes the RFC's own alternative at 4357-4358, 'for any operational or technical reason'.
