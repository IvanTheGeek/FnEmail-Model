# `ListExpanded`

## Notes

Always paired with ReturnPathRewrittenToListAdministrator — the pairing is what makes it a list rather than an alias, and neither event is complete without the other, which is a real argument for merging them that a facilitator should hear before rejecting. Rejected alternative names: MailingListRedistributed, ListExploded, MembershipSubstitutedIntoEnvelope. §3.9.2 heading confirmed at line 1718. RFC ambiguity worth recording so nobody re-derives it: at lines 1727-1730 the List subsection refers to itself as "forwarding" — "the key difference between handling aliases (Section 3.9.1) and forwarding (this subsection)" — seven lines after declaring that a list operates "by "redistribution" rather than by "forwarding"" (1720-1721). The same sentence appears verbatim a second time at §4.4 lines 3289-3291, where "this subsection" resolves to Trace Information instead, and §3.4 (heading line 1162) is separately titled "Forwarding for Address Correction or Updating". "This subsection" is unresolvable in both places; read it as "list redistribution". No RFC 2119 keyword on the expansion itself.
