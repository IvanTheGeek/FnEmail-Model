## Notes

Carried from the inherited candidate with §2.3.5 lines 716-718 added to the refs. Strength: plain indicative, no MUST, SHOULD or MAY — yet it changes which name every later step uses, so the substitution is a fact worth recording. 'processed as if it were the initial name' means a restart, not a redirect: the MX lookup begins again from the canonical name. Nothing here bounds chain length or detects a CNAME cycle: 🟥. Do NOT fuse with the prohibited case — a CNAME reached from the *data field of an MX RR* is a defect, not a chase, and is carried as MailExchangerTargetFoundUnresolvable. Precondition nobody wrote down: AliasRecordPublished, carried below. Rejected alternative names: CnameFollowed, AliasChased, CanonicalNameSubstituted. Which project screen would have killed it: the four altitude tiers would exile it to Infrastructure. ⚠️ FIELD HYGIENE PASS: nothing struck; no fabricated identity key was present. Both fields are the before and the after of the substitution and neither is derivable from the other. queried_name takes the walk scene's foo.com; canonical_name is labeled illustrative because RFC 5321 gives no worked CNAME instance anywhere — it states the rule at 3838-3839 and supplies no example, which is worth noting given rule 8.
