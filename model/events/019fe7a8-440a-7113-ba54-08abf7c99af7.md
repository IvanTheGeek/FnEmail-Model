## Notes

Strength: MAY, qualified by "when necessary" (line 1549). Note the explicit license to override §6.4 Compensating for Irregularities (heading verified at line 4074) — the RFC names the prohibition it is suspending, which is unusual and must be preserved in the model. The inspection of the body and the interpretation of the local-part are READS and go to set_aside; the rewrite is the event. Unresolved tension worth a 🟥 hotspot: §3.7.1 licenses interpreting the local-part while §2.3.11 lines 847-849 makes that a MUST-level trespass, and the RFC never reconciles them. Cross-reference: the relay chapter's MessageDataModifiedByRelay is the same act with the opposite verdict; AddressCorrectedToFqdn (§6.4, message-repair chapter) is the correction §3.7.1 is suspending — three events, one act, three different verdicts by role. Rejected alternative names: GatewayHeaderTranslationApplied, HeaderFieldTranslatedAcrossBoundary. Which project screen would have killed it: inbound-only scope, responsibility-boundary cutoff.

FIELD HYGIENE PASS: struck message_ref (example f2C8D14) — no such key exists in RFC 5321; the header field name plus its before-and-after values identify the rewrite, and gateway_host stands in for the absent identity key. Four fields is the method's ceiling and they are kept only because a rewrite is unreadable without both values and unlocatable without the field name.
