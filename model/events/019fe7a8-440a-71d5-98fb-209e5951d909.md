## Where RFC 5321 says so

| Section | Lines | Text |
|:--|:--|:--|
| §6.4 | 4078-4083 | *"The debate as to whether a well- behaved SMTP receiver or relay should reject a malformed message, attempt to pass it on unchanged, or attempt to repair it to increase the odds of successful delivery (or subsequent reply) began almost with the dawn of structured network mail and shows no signs of abating."* |
| §6.4 | 4086-4096 | *"Advocates of "repair" or "deliver no matter what" argue that users prefer that mail go through it if at all possible and that there are significant market pressures in that direction."* |

## Notes

RECOVERED BY THE RE-SWEEP, the middle arm of the §6.4 debate and the one a relay is effectively pushed toward, since MUST NOT applies to repair (4140-4141) and rejection is contested — so "pass it on unchanged" is the only arm a relay can take without either violating a MUST NOT or picking a side in an unsettled debate. That deduction is worth recording because the RFC never states it. NO NORMATIVE STRENGTH; the second quotation is the only place in RFC 5321 where market pressure is named as a force acting against conformance, which is unusual enough to preserve. The quotation at 4086-4096 spans a page break in the source and reads oddly — "users prefer that mail go through it if at all possible" — that is the RFC's own wording, kept verbatim under rule 2, not a transcription slip. Deliberately NOT merged with MalformedMessageRejected: a refusal is not an acceptance, and the two arms of the debate are the whole point. Rejected alternative name: MessageDeliveredDespiteMalformation.

FIELD HYGIENE PASS (chapter 14): message_id struck — RFC 5321 supplies no message key, section 4.4's ID clause at line 3336 is an optional trace-header clause that may hold a queue id rather than a msg-id, and this event's subject is precisely a message whose header section does not conform, so a message-id may be one of the things missing. Identity is the message on this hop, recorded by section 4.4's By-domain clause at line 3319, which is what forwarding_host carries; its value is corrected from mx.bar.com to bar.com per Appendix D lines 4881 and 5064. malformation KEPT, sourced to line 4133.
