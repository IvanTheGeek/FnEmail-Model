## Notes

RECOVERED BY THE RE-SWEEP as the violated arm of the SHOULD NOT at 3233-3234; the inherited set cited the sentence in two events' notes and gave it no row. Keep it because it is the only stated *cause* of the condition that ReturnPathHeaderStrippedBeforeDelivery and MultipleReturnPathsPresentAtDelivery exist to clean up, and a chapter that carries only cleanups leaves the mess unexplained. Strength: SHOULD NOT for the originator, against a MAY for the deliverer to fix it — the spec assigns a weak prohibition upstream and weak permission downstream, and nothing in between is obliged to do anything, which is why the condition persists in practice. The relay in the middle is forbidden even to look (3234-3237). Note the asymmetry with the trace chain: a stray Received: line must be preserved, a stray Return-Path may be deleted — the two provenance headers have opposite integrity rules and §4.4 never remarks on it. Worth a 🟥. FnEmail screens: the inbound-only scope and the responsibility-boundary cutoff; the originating system is two hops outside anything the model sees. ⚠️ FIELD HYGIENE PASS: struck the fabricated `message_ref` identity key — RFC 5321 supplies no message identifier and f2C8D14 is this project's minted queue id. Identity is the carried Return-Path, which is fitting: the offending header is the only evidence the violation ever happened, and it travels with the message rather than with any session.
