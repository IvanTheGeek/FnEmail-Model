## Notes

Strength: MAY (1191-1192 for forwarding at all, 1193-1194 for the silent variant). The state change is twofold and both halves matter: the recipient is now bound for a destination the sender did not name, AND the sender was deliberately not told. The withholding is the whole point of §3.4, which is why forwarded_to must be on the payload even though the wire never carries it — an internal/external split that a walk with real data would test directly (the client sees only 250 OK after RCPT TO:\<Green@foo.com> and can form no belief about xyz.com). Paired arm, same MAY exercised the other way: CorrectedDestinationDisclosed. Opposite branch of the section: RecipientRejectedWithForwardingAddress and RecipientRejectedWithoutAddressInformation, under "Alternately" at line 1198. The actual onward send is a separate later fact, recovered here as MessageRelayedToCorrectedAddress. Rejected alternative names: SilentForwardApplied, RecipientRedirectedWithoutNotice. Screens: the "one event per command" design target (RCPT already yields one event in the twelve-slice model), the inbound-only scope, and the responsibility-boundary cutoff. Citations fixed: inherited candidate cited 1192-1194 for a quote that begins at 1193, and 1171-1174 for a quote that ends at 1173.

⚠️ FIELD HYGIENE PASS (fields and notes only): struck two fields. transaction (TransactionId, f2C8D14) — RFC 5321 supplies no transaction identifier; a grep for "transaction id" and "transaction identifier" over rfc5321.txt returns nothing, and f2C8D14 appears nowhere in the RFC, not even in Appendix D. It is this project's own minted queue id, traceable to the walk. What carries identity here is position in the session: this is the RCPT under the MAIL FROM currently open on the channel, and while the session lives the channel is the identity — a fabricated key hid the fact that the spec offers no other. reply_code (250) — struck as the rendering of a decision rather than data the server holds; the method's no-computed-values rule reaches it, and the event's own name already says which arm of lines 1191-1194 was taken. Sourced: addressed_mailbox at rfc5321.txt:4897; forwarded_to is composed from the RFC's Green (4884) and xyz.com (1940) and the address itself is not in the RFC. Note that forwarded_to survives on exactly the argument the notes above make — it is internal state, never on the wire — and the pass did not disturb it, because striking it would have deleted the half of the event that makes silent forwarding silent.
