## Notes

RECOVERED BY THE RE-SWEEP — named inside idx 283's notes as configuration with a conventional value of 100 and never given a row. It earns one for the same reason TraceTimeFormatConfigured does: the rejection_threshold field on MailLoopDetected has no origin otherwise, and a wall where a number appears in an event payload with nothing that put it there is a wall with a hole in it. Strength: SHOULD, and softened twice over — 'large' is unquantified, 'normally at least 100' is a convention rather than a floor, and the whole sentence is conditional on the server 'using this technique'. So three conforming servers may hold three different thresholds, or none, and all three are right. That variability is the point: a message that loops through one operator's threshold survives another's. Sibling configuration events elsewhere in this chapter: TraceTimeFormatConfigured. Consuming event: MailLoopDetected, LoopingMessageRejected.
