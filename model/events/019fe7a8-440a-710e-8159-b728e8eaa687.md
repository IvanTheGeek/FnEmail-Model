## Notes

RECOVERED BY THE RE-SWEEP. The merged list cited §3.5.3 lines 1391-1394 inside RecipientRejectedWithForwardingAddress's notes but never gave the case its own event, so the entire non-transaction arm of 251/551 was missing: no MAIL FROM, no RCPT, no message, no transaction — the disclosure happens to anyone who can open a session and type VRFY. That makes it the chapter's worst disclosure case and the one AddressUpdateDisclosureRestricted most needs to cover. Stream root is Session, not Recipient or Transaction, precisely because none of those exist here. Strength: the sentence carries no keyword of its own; it inherits §3.4's MAYs by reference. Marked likely because the RFC states the applicability, not the act. Read-test tension, recorded not resolved: VRFY and EXPN are set aside as pure lookups by the guest-viewed-calendar test, and this event is the disclosure CONSEQUENCE of one — the same judgment the §3.4-3.5 sweep made for MailboxExistenceDisclosedViaVrfy and MailingListMembershipDisclosedViaExpn, and it should be settled once for all three, not three times. Rejected alternative names: ForwardingIntentDisclosedByVerification, VrfyAnsweredWithForwardPath. Screens: the consumer test, "one event per command", and inbound-only.

⚠️ FIELD HYGIENE PASS (fields and notes only): struck two fields, and the first strike is the chapter's sharpest finding. session (SessionId, f2C8D14) — RFC 5321 supplies NO session identifier: a grep for "session id" and "session identifier" over rfc5321.txt returns nothing, and f2C8D14 is this project's own minted queue id, absent from the RFC body and from Appendix D. The absence bites hardest exactly HERE, because the notes above chose Session as this event's stream root on the express grounds that no transaction and no recipient exist yet — and the spec gives that root no key at all. What carries identity while a session lives is the channel itself; there is nothing else, and a fabricated session_id concealed the one structural fact this event was recovered to expose. A later step choosing stream roots must weigh that: Session is the right root and it is a keyless one. reply_code (251) struck as the rendering of a decision — §3.5.3 rfc5321.txt:1391-1394 names 251 and 551 together and inherits §3.4's MAYs by reference, so the code selects the event rather than sitting on it.
