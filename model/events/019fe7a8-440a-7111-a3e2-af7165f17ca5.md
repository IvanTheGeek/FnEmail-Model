## Notes

The permission boundary is the modelable fact: the same physical act is a violation for a relay (§3.6.3 lines 1524-1526, MUST NOT act upon header section or body) and permitted for a gateway, so the ACTOR ROLE is load-bearing on whether the event is legal. That contrast is the best hotspot in the chapter and belongs on the wall paired with the relay chapter's MessageDataModifiedByRelay — cross-referenced, deliberately NOT merged, because a refusal-by-role and a permission-by-role are opposite verdicts on one act. Strength: NONE — "may require" is descriptive. Rejected alternative names: GatewayTransformationApplied, MessageAdaptedForForeignEnvironment. transformation_kind is a fact about what was done, not a computed classification; if a facilitator finds it too coarse, split by kind rather than deriving it. Which project screen would have killed it: inbound-only scope, responsibility-boundary cutoff, altitude tiers.
