## Notes

THREE candidates merged: ServiceUnavailableAnnounced (§3.8 sweep), ShutdownNoticeSent (§3.8 sweep) and ServiceShutdownAnnounced (§4.2/4.3 sweep). The ShutdownNoticeSent candidate explicitly flagged the merge for a facilitator; it is taken here. Rejected alternative names: ShutdownNoticeSent, ServiceShutdownAnnounced, ShutdownReplyIssued, FourTwentyOneSent. The merge must NOT hide the strength difference between the two paragraphs it spans: at lines 1651-1655 issuing 421 is an exception to a MUST NOT, so it is PERMITTED; at lines 1666-1668 it is a SHOULD, so it is REQUIRED-with-an-escape. The RFC also says 'a line containing a 421 response code', not 'a 421 reply' — deliberately weaker, since a dying process may manage a write but not a proper reply sequence. issued_asynchronously is the field that makes this reply unlike every other in the model: the spec explicitly licenses a server reply that answers no command, which breaks the request/response pairing the twelve-slice model is built on and is exactly why the one-event-per-command target rejects it. Its text is machine-parsable by §4.2's carve-out. The precondition is ServiceShutdownDetected or ServiceForciblyShutDown; the client-side arrival is ShutdownNoticeReceived, genuinely later in time. FIELD HYGIENE PASS: struck 3 fields. session_id struck: no such key in the spec. reply_code 421 struck as the rendering of an announcement the event name and meaning already carry; §4.2.2 line 2811 supplies the registered text, and §4.2 lines 2610-2612 makes 421 one of the five codes whose text a client may act on, which is what keeps reply_text and not the code. issued_asynchronously struck as derived: it is decidable entirely from whether in_reply_to_command is present, since §3.8 lines 1652-1655 licenses exactly two cases — after any command, or asynchronously from command receipt. ⚠️ The note above calls that Boolean the field that makes this reply unlike every other; the point stands and is now carried by the absence of in_reply_to_command rather than by a second field restating it.
