## Notes

RECOVERED BY THE RE-SWEEP from §4.3.2 lines 3063-3065 — the violated arm of OversizedCommandLineRejected, and no inherited candidate carried it although candidate 237's notes did mention the sentence in passing without giving it a row. The underlying obligation is a MUST receive-floor at §4.5.3.1 lines 3462-3464: the 512 is a minimum a receiver must accept, not a maximum it may choose, so any configured ceiling below 512 makes every rejection under it a violation. That is why SizeLimitConfigured below can record a non-conforming configuration and this event is its runtime consequence. Note the source's own slip, quoted as written per rule 2: "512 characters" here, where §4.5.3.1.4 says "512 octets" — characters and octets are the same only until an extension uses multi-octet characters, which §4.5.3.1 lines 3479-3482 explicitly anticipates. Rejected alternative name: SizeFloorBreached.

FIELD HYGIENE PASS (chapter 14): session_id struck — RFC 5321 names no session, transaction or connection identifier, and f2C8D14 is nowhere in the RFC; identity here is position in the session. reply_code struck as the decision rendered and as a constant implied by the meaning. line_octets KEPT and it is the whole violation: 310 is below the 512-octet receive floor, and the measured length is the only fact that establishes non-conformance. This is the clearest case in the chapter of a measurement being genuine payload rather than padding, which is why it survives where limit_octets did not.
