## Notes

Carried from the inherited candidate, with D.3's worked instance added. Strength: MUST to discard, SHOULD to try what remains — the two halves of one sentence pair carry different strengths, and citing either alone misreports the requirement. WORDING TRAP worth recording: 'higher-numbered' means LESS preferred (3884-3885, 'Lower numbers are more preferred than higher ones'), so reading 'higher' as 'more preferred' inverts the rule and reintroduces the very loop it exists to cut. remaining_exchangers is not a computed field in the sense the kit forbids — it is the surviving candidate set the next attempt runs against, and the discard is what created it; a later step may still choose to carry only discarded_exchangers. Rejected alternative names: SelfAndWorseCandidatesTruncated, MxListTruncatedAtSelf, WorsePreferenceCandidatesRemoved. Which project screen would have killed it: no screen names it specifically; the twelve-slice model simply has nowhere to put it. ⚠️ FIELD HYGIENE PASS: struck remaining_exchangers, taking the option the inherited note itself left open — it is the sorted candidate list minus discarded_exchangers, which is a computation over facts already recorded and therefore a read model's job. The inherited defense is left above so the disagreement is visible. threshold_preference and discarded_exchangers are the discard itself and neither is recoverable from the other. No fabricated identity key was present. Both examples are labeled illustrative because RFC 5321 states no preference numbers and D.3 names no backup exchanger host.
