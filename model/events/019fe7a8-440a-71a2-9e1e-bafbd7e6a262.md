## Notes

RECOVERED BY THE RE-SWEEP. Sweep #77 named this sibling in its notes ('NextHopChannelRefused / NextHopUnreachable - the failure arm') and had no slot for it; the cap ate it. A channel that never opened is a different fact from one that opened and then failed mid-transfer, and only the first is fixable by trying a lower-preference MX. Strength: none - 3.6.2 states only the success path, which is itself worth a hotspot, because the whole failure side of the onward leg is specified nowhere in this span and arrives only obliquely at 1495 as 'cannot be delivered for some other reason'. Rejected names: NextHopUnreachable, OnwardConnectionFailed. Feeds DeliveryAttemptFailed. Screens: inbound-only scope, responsibility-boundary cutoff, consumer test. --- FIELD HYGIENE: struck relay_host, refused_at and next_hop_ip (same role error and same lookup-derivation as in NextHopChannelEstablished). The surviving value is genuinely sourced to a failed connection in the RFC's own scene: Appendix D.3 line 4998, 'It attempts to open a connection to xyz.com and fails.' - the one place the spec instantiates this arm.
