## Notes

NO normative keyword on the 4-5 days — "generally needs to be" (3709-3710) is advisory despite reading as firm, and this is exactly the MUST-that-was-a-SHOULD trap rule 3 records. Merged from two DeliveryGivenUp records and from the timeout arm of MessageBouncedAsUndeliverable; the notification arm of that candidate is deliberately NOT merged — constructing and mailing the undeliverable notice (§3.6.3 1496-1499, a real MUST) is the bounce chapter's event and cross-referenced only. Rejected names: MessageBouncedAsUndeliverable, QueueLifetimeExpired, RetryScheduleExhausted. §2.2.3 601-604 gives the bounce timeout its only illustrative figure, three days, and constrains only its RELATIONSHIP to the fallback timeout. Dropped as derived: attempts_made. Opposite event: QueuedMessageTransmitted. Screens: inbound-only scope, responsibility-boundary cutoff. ⚠️ FIELD HYGIENE PASS: STRUCK first_queued_at — it is MessageQueuedForDelivery.queued_at restated, so it is derived from the stream rather than new here. STRUCK give_up_after_days — a configured parameter, and 3713-3714 makes configurability a MUST, so it belongs on RetryParametersConfigured where it already sits; copying it onto every give-up padded the payload and would let two records of the same configuration disagree. forward_path is kept as the one fact beyond identity: it is what could not be delivered, and the bounce the next chapter builds is about it.
