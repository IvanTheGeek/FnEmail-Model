## Notes

Recovered by the re-sweep. MAY strength on the shorter budget (3710-3713), but the silence is a MUST NOT (3682-3683) — so this event is terminal in a way DeliveryGivenUp is not: it can produce no bounce, and the originator of the original message never learns that the news never arrived. That asymmetry is why it is a separate event and not a DeliveryGivenUp with a flag. Sibling: ErrorNotificationSuppressedToPreventLoop, carried by the §4.5.5 span in another chapter. Rejected names: BounceGivenUp, NotificationRetryBudgetExhausted. Screens: inbound-only scope, responsibility-boundary cutoff, and the consumer test G1, since by construction nothing downstream reads it. ⚠️ FIELD HYGIENE PASS: STRUCK abandoned_at as a bare occurrence stamp. undeliverable_to is kept and is well grounded: §4.5.5 3793-3795 says notifications "are sent to the reverse-path of the previous mail message", so the address is genuinely the abandoned notification's own forward path, and the walk scene's \<Smith@bar.com> is the reverse-path of the original message in that scene. queue_entry_id kept on §4.4's ID-clause ground (rfc5321.txt:3336); f2C8D14 is this project's minted value, not the RFC's.
