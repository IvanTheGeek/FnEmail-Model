# `SourceRouteHonored`

## Notes

RECOVERED BY THE RE-SWEEP, and named in this chapter's charter as the other half of 'source route encountered and stripped'. The inherited set had only the stripping arm. Honoring the route is the arm the SHOULD at 1432 discourages but does not forbid, and 1952-1956 show it explicitly as a permitted choice with worked envelope commands. Strength: conformant-but-discouraged - MAY ignore / SHOULD ignore means honoring is legal. 4.1.1.3 gives FOUR legal outcomes for the same source-routed RCPT - direct to the final destination (1946-1950), on to the first routed hop keeping the full route (1952-1956), on to a later hop with the route shortened (1967-1970), or refusing entirely with 550 (1972-1975) - and 1972 says 'Attempting to use relaying this way is now strongly discouraged.' Four outcomes from one input, all discretionary, is what this event makes visible. Further constraint: a server MUST NOT guess at shortcuts within the route (#312, Appendix F.2). Rejected names: RouteFollowedToNextHop, SourceRouteUsed, MessageRelayedAlongSourceRoute. --- FIELD HYGIENE: struck offered_path - the input is already recorded by SourceRoutedAddressAccepted. The two survivors are exactly what distinguishes the RFC's own alternatives: to hosta.int with the route intact (1952-1956), or to jkl.org with the route shortened to \<@jkl.org:userc@d.bar.org> (1967-1970). Neither is derivable from the other.
