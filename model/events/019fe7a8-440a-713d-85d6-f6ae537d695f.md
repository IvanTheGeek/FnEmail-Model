## Notes

RECOVERED BY THE RE-SWEEP as the violated arm of the SHOULD at 1689-1690 — every MUST, SHOULD and MAY has two arms and the inherited set carried neither of these. Marked doubtful because it records a configuration that was never made rather than one that was, which is the weakest kind of Step 1 candidate: the honest reading is that AddressExpansionSupportEnabled simply lists what is supported and this event is its complement. Kept anyway because the consequence is sharp and observable — a list address on such a host is deliverable to nobody, and no other event in the chapter explains that outcome. Rejected alternative names: ListModelUnsupported, ExpansionModelOmitted, PartialExpansionSupportConfigured. Screens: altitude tiers, consumer test G1, and the condemned status register would reach it by shape. FIELD HYGIENE PASS: nothing struck. Both fields are sourced — the host from Appendix D.1 line 4882, the model name from the SHOULD's own words at 1689-1690 — and neither is derived from another event: unsupported_model is not the complement of AddressExpansionSupportEnabled.supported_models computed after the fact but the model this operator chose to leave out. That said, the complement reading recorded above is precisely the objection to the whole candidate, and the hygiene pass leaves it exactly where it stood: doubtful, with both readings visible. Nothing here was fabricated, so nothing collapsed.
