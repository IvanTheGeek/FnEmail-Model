## Where RFC 5321 says so

| Section | Lines | Text |
|:--|:--|:--|
| §4.1.1.4 | 1998-2001 | *"The mail data may contain any of the 128 ASCII character codes, although experience has indicated that use of control characters other than SP, HT, CR, and LF may cause problems and SHOULD be avoided when possible."* |
| §4.5.2 | 3441-3443 | *"The mail data may contain any of the 128 ASCII characters. All characters are to be delivered to the recipient's mailbox, including spaces, vertical and horizontal tabs, and other control characters."* |

## Notes

RECOVERED BY THE RE-SWEEP. Strength: SHOULD, and doubly hedged ("when possible"). The pair of citations is the point and is why this earns an event rather than a footnote: §4.1.1.4 discourages these characters while §4.5.2 line 3442-3443 requires that they be delivered anyway if they arrive — "including spaces, vertical and horizontal tabs, and other control characters". So the spec advises the sender not to send them and forbids the receiver to strip them, which means a real state change with two conforming parties and no defensive action available. No opposite arm exists: a server that acts on this SHOULD has violated §4.5.2. Siblings: BareLineTerminatorTransmitted (the CR/LF case, which is a MUST NOT rather than a SHOULD) and IrreversibleStorageTransformApplied (the store that silently drops them). Screens: the altitude tiers (Infrastructure) and the consumer test. Rejected alternative name: DiscouragedControlCharacterSent.

FIELD HYGIENE PASS: struck one of two. transaction_id is fabricated — RFC 5321 supplies no transaction key; identity is the channel, and this event's anchor is the content interval §2.3.9 defines. control_character survives as the one fact that makes the event meaningful: which character it was is what separates a SHOULD-discouraged octet from an ordinary one. The value NUL is illustrative — §4.1.1.4 line 2000 names only the four exceptions SP, HT, CR and LF, never a specific offender — but the notation "(hex value 00)" copies the RFC's own form at rfc5321.txt:766.
